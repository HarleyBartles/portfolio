This article already has a public stub at `src/client/src/data/content/writing/2026-08-07-context-is-not-state.md`. ## Article responsibility

The article's governing territory is durable, governed project truth: conversation can carry working context, but a decision, constraint or result that now governs work needs an inspectable home outside that conversation.

Keep its job distinct from its neighbours:

- **Review Graph** governs a workflow and its transitions.
- **Pass references, not paragraphs** concerns compact transfer of durable material between workers.
- **Context** establishes the prerequisite: there must be governed material worth referencing in the first place.

The article should make one concrete, arguable engineering point, not wear a context-window explainer or a catalogue of artifacts as an article coat.

## Discovery and grooming constraints

- Find the employer-safe or public story that gives the premise stakes. A slogan about files and chat does not earn an article by itself.
- Establish what Harley personally did, observed, inferred or remains uncertain about. Preserve the distinction.
- Keep materialised state, authority, lifecycle, freshness and recovery legible without claiming that a file is automatically correct merely because it persists.
- Try one deliberate curveball only after enough evidence exists to test whether the argument becomes materially sharper. Harley decides whether it survives.
- Keep the prose natural: contractions, normal paragraphs, no one-sentence staccato, no filler reversal frames, and no abstract agentic sermon.
- Do not decide publication, build the page, choose imagery or run production validation. Local Codex handles later presentation and admission.
