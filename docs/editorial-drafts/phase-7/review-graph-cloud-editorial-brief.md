## Time-box and context-rot protocol

At the start:

1. Record the UTC start, three-hour target and five-hour hard stop in the checkpoint.
2. Read the existing checkpoint before asking Harley anything.
3. Ask the smallest question that can materially change the article.

During discovery:

- Save a checkpoint at every natural break and never leave more than 45 minutes of useful conversation only in chat.
- Commit and push the checkpoint to this same PR branch after each material batch of answers.
- Give every settled answer a stable `RG-A###` ID. Before asking a question, search the checkpoint for the answer or a materially equivalent one.
- Reopen an answered question only when Harley contradicts it, new repository evidence changes it, or the manuscript exposes a precise ambiguity. Record why it was reopened.
- Keep factual answers, editorial decisions, voice language, anti-inferences, private material and unresolved questions visibly separate.
- Preserve useful rejected material under “Cutting-room material from this article” or route it into the cross-article thread ledger. Do not make a strong discovery disappear because it doesn't fit this manuscript.
- Redraft regularly in `docs/editorial-drafts/phase-7/review-graph-cloud-working-draft.md`. Save after each settled narrative section or at least once per hour. Replace the working manuscript freely; do not rewrite the append-only answer history.

At five hours:

1. Stop asking questions.
2. Make one final coherent manuscript pass using only settled facts.
3. Update the checkpoint's baton status to `Ready for local production`, `Needs one named fact` or `Not yet publishable`.
4. Commit and push the manuscript, checkpoint and any cross-article ledger additions to this PR branch.

## Discovery order

The fastest route is consequence before machinery:

1. What failure or doubt made the meaning of green matter?
2. Why was a graph the right correction to the checklist failure?
3. Which boundaries in version one genuinely worked?
4. Where did trust fail despite those boundaries?
5. Why does the replacement design attack coverage, independence, provenance and snapshot identity rather than add more reviewers?
6. What does Harley now believe a trustworthy completion claim owes its reader?
7. What remains unimplemented, uncertain, private or open to disagreement?

Do not interrogate Harley with this whole list at once. Follow the story and keep the checkpoint current.

## Editorial bar

The manuscript must eventually do all of the following:

- lead with a consequence or correction rather than graph anatomy;
- keep implemented behaviour, observed weakness and planned behaviour unmistakably separate;
- make conventional engineering judgement legible before teaching node names;
- show a rejected or superseded approach and why it failed;
- give the hiring manager a fast reason to shortlist;
- give the cynical architect a narrow claim they can agree with quickly;
- preserve Harley's contractions, directness, technical confidence, dry humour and occasional rough edge;
- avoid em dashes, stock reversal frames, repeated one-sentence paragraphs, slogan stacks, symmetrical AI cadence and unexplained noun lists;
- use public source links as receipts, not as a substitute for a story; and
- remain unpublished working copy until Harley and local Sol approve production treatment.

Do not productionise the React page, choose principal imagery, alter admission or make the PR ready for merge. Local Sol and Harley will take the settled copy into visual production.
