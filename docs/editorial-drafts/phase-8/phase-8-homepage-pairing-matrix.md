# Phase 8 homepage writing/Patch pairing matrix

**Status:** Proposed initial current-corpus matrix for Harley review. This file applies the publishing-time pairing contract in `phase-8-homepage-decision-record.md`. It is editorial evidence, not runtime configuration and not an implementation plan.

## Fixed homepage evidence this matrix must not duplicate

The current homepage design room already treats these as fixed jobs:

- **Opening professional signal:** Harley Bartles, full-stack software engineer, `5 years at The Access Group`, plus one bounded consequence-bearing signal that responsibility extends beyond straightforward implementation.
- **Agent Asset Marketplace:** inspectable agentic-engineering infrastructure; reusable engineering guidance with local repository custody.
- **Wild Bunch:** serious software engineering outside the AI bubble; seeded worlds, exact replay, persistent generated towns and conventional architectural judgement.

A writing/Patch pair must add a different dimension or create a materially richer editorial relationship. It is not enough for both pieces to be good.

## Current Patch lessons

| Patch adventure | Operative lesson for homepage pairing |
| --- | --- |
| `goldilocks` | Good guidance is not maximum guidance. Enough relevant structure supports the next confident decision; too much becomes another problem and too little leaves the route undefined. |
| `sorcerers-apprentice` | Delegation needs a boundary around authority, depth, tools, data, reporting and escalation; permission alone lets the work escape its intended topology. |
| `identity-emporium` | Preparation should shape capability around the actual task without becoming a costume-script or disappearing altogether; judgement keeps the role relevant once work begins. |
| `tournament-of-reasonable-defaults` | Apparently clear instructions can hide unresolved boundaries, standards, choices and acceptance conditions. A valid completion requires the finish line to be agreed rather than merely plausible. |
| `lawful-heist` | A legitimate route across a protected boundary needs provenance, pressure, authority, a bounded decision, recovery and audit. Done well, execution becomes boring because the difficult judgement happened before the move. |

The longer Patch routes remain truthfully labelled with their upstream production states. This matrix judges the substantial public portfolio experiences that currently exist on the site, not whether their source project has completed every planned upstream artefact.

## Proposed active pair set

### P01 — Vibe coding × Tournament of Reasonable Defaults

**Article argument:** a clickable first result is useful, but appearance of completion is not durability.

**Patch lesson:** an instruction or route can look clear while the finish line and acceptance condition remain undefined.

**Why they belong together:** both examine the gap between `looks like success` and `has earned success`, at different scales. The article works at product lifecycle scale: prototype versus durable system. Tournament works at task/acceptance scale: plausible completion versus agreed completion. The pair therefore reinforces one judgement without retelling one story.

**What it adds:** product and requirements judgement. Marketplace proves reusable agentic infrastructure; Wild Bunch proves conventional engineering depth; this pair proves scepticism about apparent completion.

**Why it is not redundant:** neither the opening nor the two fixed projects owns the distinction between a plausible finish and an earned finish.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** quiet authored prose about the long road after a convincing demo can give way to the visually literal starting lines, standards, mazes and finish lines of Tournament.

**Rejected alternative:** Vibe coding × Identity Emporium. Both can support a broad `use the tool for what it is actually for` reading, but the relationship is looser and needs more explanatory homepage copy. Tournament makes the article's completion boundary legible without inventing connective prose.

### P02 — Why ADRs? × The Lawful Heist Crew

**Article argument:** a future engineer should inherit the argument and evidence behind a decision, not merely the implementation and its vocabulary.

**Patch lesson:** lawful action becomes trustworthy when provenance, pressure, authority, decision, recovery and audit remain inspectable through the route.

**Why they belong together:** `Why ADRs?` is retrospective custody: preserve why a decision was made so the next engineer can challenge it fairly. Lawful Heist is prospective custody: make the route citeable, pressure-testable, authorised, recoverable and auditable before execution. One looks backward from inheritance; the other looks forward from permission.

**What it adds:** institutional memory, provenance and transferable engineering judgement. The pair widens the homepage beyond code, AI tooling and game architecture into the question of what another engineer can safely inherit.

**Why it is not redundant:** Wild Bunch can demonstrate architecture, but this pair explains how architectural reasoning survives people and time. Marketplace can expose assets, but it does not own decision history.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** the article's decision-record register can resolve into the Heist folder, assent markers and ordered specialist sequence. The shared object is a record, but the forms and jobs differ.

**Rejected alternative:** Why ADRs? × Tournament. Tournament usefully records multiple plausible choices before the decision, but it mostly concerns ambiguity before action. Heist carries the stronger full custody chain: provenance, authority, decision, recovery and audit.

### P03 — The right test isn't your favourite test × Tournament of Reasonable Defaults

**Article argument:** move the observation point until the test can falsify the claim you are actually about to trust.

**Patch lesson:** success is meaningless when the boundary, standard or acceptance condition being judged has not been made explicit.

**Why they belong together:** Tournament sits one step upstream of the testing article. It asks whether the success condition is defined at all; the article asks what evidence can genuinely examine that condition once defined. Together they form a claim -> acceptance -> observation chain without pretending every problem is a testing problem.

**What it adds:** evidence strategy and acceptance discipline across normal software, UI, integration work and agentic systems.

**Why it is not redundant:** Marketplace demonstrates engineered assets but does not prove a general evidence philosophy. Wild Bunch demonstrates replay and architecture but not how Harley chooses an observation boundary.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** the article's changing observation points can hand into Tournament's changing event shapes: date boundary, named standard, multiple plausible prizes and agreed finish line.

**Rejected alternative:** Testing × Lawful Heist. Silk, Rollback and Receipt create an attractive pressure/recovery/audit mapping, but it becomes too taxonomic and risks using Heist as an illustrated checklist for the article. Tournament keeps the pair about the meaning of evidence rather than matching test vocabulary to characters.

### P04 — If you write a loop, don't be surprised when your agent starts looping × The Sorcerer's Apprentice

**Article argument:** when an agent is asked to surrender global workflow reasoning to executable routing, that routing has to deserve the authority through lawful transitions, recovery and honest blocking.

**Patch lesson:** delegated work needs explicit bounds around authority, depth, tools, data and escalation or a sensible handoff can become uncontrolled multiplication.

**Why they belong together:** both concern what happens after control is handed away from the immediate human decision point. The graph article governs **where work may move next**; Sorcerer's Apprentice governs **how far delegated authority may propagate**. They are different control surfaces with the same engineering demand: the boundary has to be executable enough that the consumer need not rescue it by intuition.

**What it adds:** control-plane engineering and the consequences of delegating judgement to machinery.

**Why it is not redundant:** Marketplace proves that agent guidance can become reusable infrastructure; this pair attacks the harder question of when that infrastructure is trustworthy enough to control execution.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** an explicit graph and deterministic router can give way to the fairytale's initially bounded delegation exploding into a crowd, then resolving around a visible delegation policy.

**Rejected alternative:** Graph × Tournament. Both can be described through routes and finish states, but that similarity is too geometric. Sorcerer's Apprentice matches the real authority-transfer problem rather than the diagram shape.

### P05 — I made agentic engineering harder than it needed to be × The Lawful Heist Crew

**Article argument:** complexity, persistent roles and governance should survive only when they protect a real boundary and can explain the rent they pay.

**Patch lesson:** six specialists are justified because each owns a materially different responsibility in making one protected route legitimate and recoverable.

**Why they belong together:** this is the strongest deliberate contrast in the current corpus. The article removes an unearned standing organisation around a novel that only needed three domains. Lawful Heist builds a crew because provenance, pressure, authority, decision, recovery and audit genuinely are separate jobs. The pair proves that Harley is not ideologically for or against orchestration; he distinguishes decorative organisation from earned organisation.

**What it adds:** proportion and earned complexity. It makes the homepage harder to dismiss as either `agents everywhere` enthusiasm or simplistic anti-process minimalism.

**Why it is not redundant:** Marketplace shows reusable infrastructure, but not the negative judgement required to delete machinery that no longer pays rent. Wild Bunch demonstrates earned architectural complexity in a different domain; this pair makes the decision principle explicit through two opposing agentic examples.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** the article can close on machinery getting out of the way; Heist can then reveal six roles that visibly do earn their place, each leaving a different mark on the shared folder.

**Rejected alternative:** Harder × Goldilocks. `Too much becomes another problem` is true of both, but that is precisely why the match is weak: the Patch piece would mostly restate the article. Heist creates contrast and demonstrates the boundary of the article's argument.

### P06 — Provisioning is not accumulation × Identity Emporium

**Article argument:** keep capability available, but activate the method and context relevant to the current work instead of loading the whole environment into every worker's attention.

**Patch lesson:** useful preparation is shaped by the job; costume without judgement becomes a script, while skipping preparation leaves the worker under-equipped.

**Why they belong together:** both reject the false choice between `load everything` and `prepare nothing`. Provisioning operates at environment/capability level; Identity Emporium operates at role/task level. A capability can exist in the workshop without being in the worker's hands until the task makes it relevant.

**What it adds:** environment design and task-shaped preparation, without relying on another abstract agent-governance claim.

**Why it is not redundant:** Marketplace proves the value of reusable assets. This pair proves the second-order judgement required to decide when a reusable asset or role should actually enter context.

**Editorial/visual handoff:** the article's workshop/provisioning language can resolve into the Emporium's visible role kits and the three-way comparison between scripted costume, under-preparation and task-shaped preparation.

**Rejected alternative:** Provisioning × Goldilocks. The article itself explicitly uses Goldilocks to make its `do not carry the whole workshop` point. That makes the pairing source-valid but homepage-poor: the Patch piece would repeat an analogy already inside the article instead of adding another dimension.

## Current-composition exclusion

### `"I just write the code" is not a full sentence` — homepage-ineligible for the current composition

This is **not** a quality judgement. The article is one of the strongest direct statements of Harley's professional maturation, but that is exactly the problem for the current homepage edit.

Its governing argument — responsibility expanding around implementation while preserving legitimate ownership boundaries — substantially duplicates the first fold's fixed job: one consequence-bearing signal that Harley's responsibility extends beyond straightforward implementation. Giving the article the writing slot would spend scarce homepage space proving the opening proposition again.

The exclusion must be re-reviewed if the first fold becomes materially narrower or stops carrying that professional-responsibility job.

Plausible Patch partners exist. Identity Emporium in particular shares the article's `know enough to act, know where the role stops` territory. That does not overcome the collision with the opening.

## Pair-set pressure result

The proposed set gives every currently homepage-eligible article one strong partner without manufacturing symmetry:

- Tournament supports two different article relationships: `apparent completion versus earned completion` for Vibe, and `defined claim versus useful evidence` for Testing.
- Lawful Heist supports two deliberately different relationships: `decision custody through time` for ADRs, and `earned organisation versus theatre` for Harder.
- Sorcerer's Apprentice is the strongest current control-boundary partner for Graph.
- Identity Emporium is the strongest current task-shaped capability partner for Provisioning.
- Goldilocks has **no active pair in this initial set**. That is deliberate. Its most obvious current relationships are already too directly stated inside the writing corpus, especially Provisioning. The complete Patch route remains authoritative; homepage rotation is not a coverage quota.

This is evidence that pair cardinality must not drive selection probability. If accepted, runtime selection should remain article-normalised as already decided.
