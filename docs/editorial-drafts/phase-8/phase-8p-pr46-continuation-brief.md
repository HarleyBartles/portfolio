## Settled inputs

Typography, substrate, project-colour authority, baseline spatial grammar, evidence/imagery custody and page rhythm/density are now settled inputs. Do not keep reopening them absent a concrete contradiction exposed by integrated proof.

Evidence custody specifically means:

- source-native artifact boundary by default;
- shared captions/provenance/status immediately around the artifact;
- a local custody seam only when parallel provenance materially improves understanding and does not damage the evidence;
- no universal evidence-card/header/footer component;
- purposeful cropping may isolate the evidence-bearing region when surrounding product chrome or empty field adds no necessary context, while preserving the original source and custody;
- Wild Bunch live capture can use reproducible seed/setup state rather than depending on one existing Dustwell screenshot; Harley performs the gameplay/clicking and Cloud owns screenshot selection/capture/cropping.

Rhythm and density specifically means:

- space expresses relationship rather than one global spacing temperament;
- one claim and its evidence may form a tighter cluster;
- a real change of argument, subject or story movement earns more separation;
- authored longform keeps a stable reading pulse rather than inheriting project-page compression;
- rules may reinforce real boundaries but must not become the default hierarchy mechanism;
- exact spacing values belong to implementation/refinement unless they materially change the system's character.

The older checkpoint's four-stage list is superseded only in count by the settled stage-1 and stage-2 decisions: evidence custody and rhythm/density are closed, leaving two discovery stages. Its stop rule and integrated-proof exit criterion remain authoritative.
