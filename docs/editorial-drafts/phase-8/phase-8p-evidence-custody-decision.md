# Phase 8P stage 1 — evidence and imagery custody

**Status:** Settled in PR #46. Stage 1 is closed unless integrated proof exposes a concrete contradiction.

This decision is subordinate to the wider Phase 8P visual-language checkpoint and typography contract. It closes the evidence/imagery-custody discovery stage without authorizing implementation.

## Selected default: artifact first

**Evidence keeps its native visual boundary by default.** Shared portfolio styling establishes custody immediately around the artifact rather than wrapping unlike evidence in one universal component.

The evidence should first read as what it is: a game capture, source excerpt, decision record, document, diagram, or other receipt. Captions, provenance, status, qualification and nearby rules belong to the shared system; the artifact itself does not have to be visually normalised into house furniture.

Artifact-first does not mean provenance-optional. The relationship between evidence and its source/status/qualification must remain visually unambiguous through adjacency, ordering and rules.

## Earned special case: custody seam

A local custody seam may be introduced when provenance, status, qualification, revision or source history is independently important enough to deserve parallel reading beside the artifact.

The seam is information architecture, not decoration. It must not:

- become a repeated branded side rail;
- steal width the evidence materially needs;
- force code or other content into avoidable horizontal scrolling;
- survive at narrow widths when normal source order is clearer.

If the custody information can be understood cleanly before or after the artifact, artifact-first remains the better choice.

## Rejected default: universal evidence module

A shared header/body/footer frame for every receipt is rejected as the default evidence language. It is orderly but flattens unlike material into component-library sameness and makes project-native evidence feel inserted into a portfolio widget rather than shown on its own terms.

This does not ban every border or frame. A border/radius/frame must have a job that belongs to the artifact or its immediate reading context rather than exist because all evidence is expected to enter the same component.

## Cropping and evidence-bearing regions

Harley identified a flaw in the first evidence-custody comparison: the Dustwell source screenshot contains a large dark product/page field around the town hub, which visually dominated the comparison even though the town-hub map was the evidence under review.

**Purposeful cropping is therefore allowed and sometimes preferred.** A source capture may be cropped to the evidence-bearing region when surrounding chrome or empty field adds no necessary interpretive context and materially distorts the composition.

Guards:

- the crop must not omit context needed to understand or qualify the claim;
- custody must make clear that a crop/derivative is being shown when that fact is relevant;
- the original source capture and provenance remain preserved or linkable;
- cropping must not turn an inconvenient artifact into a misleadingly stronger receipt.

The deterministic comparison surface now crops the existing Dustwell source capture to the town-hub map for visual pressure testing rather than allowing the surrounding dark field to decide the layout.

## Wild Bunch live-capture workflow

For later integrated proof and eventual production evidence, Cloud does not need to remain constrained to the existing Dustwell screenshot.

Wild Bunch can be run locally and opened in Opera. An agent may drive a chosen seed/setup-salt state to a town hub, after which Cloud can inspect the rendered app and take/crop browser screenshots. The game's seeded architecture provides many legitimate town-hub variations rather than one canonical Dustwell composition.

This capability is useful, but it is not permission to hunt indefinitely for the prettiest procedural frame. A selected capture must earn its use by illustrating a real project/material need.

For any new Wild Bunch evidence derivative intended to survive the design room, preserve enough custody to reproduce or audit it:

- Wild Bunch repository revision;
- world seed;
- relevant setup salts/options/difficulty/entropy choices needed to reproduce the state;
- town/location identity where useful;
- raw capture identity/checksum before portfolio cropping or optimisation;
- derivative crop/optimisation receipt when the file enters the portfolio.

This turns the game's own replayable/seeded architecture into part of the evidence provenance rather than treating generated variation as decorative stock material.

## Project-specific imagery remains project-owned

Wild Bunch may still use its approved sparse tintype/emulsion-density-loss edge where the image itself supports that treatment. The custody system begins outside the project-owned image treatment; the portfolio must not replace it with a generic CSS fade or universal evidence frame.

Other projects retain the same right to source-native imagery and artifact boundaries. The shared system owns the custody relationship, not the visual identity of every receipt.

## Stage-1 stop decision

The evidence-custody question has crossed the Phase 8P stop rule:

- three materially different treatments were rendered;
- image, code and decision-record evidence were pressure-tested together;
- Harley accepted the artifact-first baseline and custody-seam special case;
- the selected direction survived a deliberate challenge around consistency/provenance;
- the Dustwell framing defect exposed a source-selection/cropping rule rather than a reason to reopen the layout decision.

Stage 1 is therefore closed. Remaining Phase 8P discovery proceeds to page rhythm/density, then shared shell/navigation, then integrated route proof.
