# Phase 8 homepage first-fold lock

**Status:** Accepted by Harley on 1 September 2026. This is the current editorial/art-direction authority for the homepage first fold. It records a forward decision; it does not rewrite the earlier wireframe or decision history.

## Five-second job

The first fold owns the reader's **five-second decision space**.

Its job is not to create sustained interest or prove the whole portfolio. Its job is to earn enough attention that the reader chooses one further interaction: scroll into the work or use the permanent site navigation.

Every visible element in this fold must therefore pay rent. Material that merely repeats another element's job is removed rather than decorated into relevance.

## Locked content hierarchy

The fold has five jobs, in this order:

1. **Permanent site navigation** — the masthead owns navigation across the site.
2. **Orientation** — `Harley Bartles · Full-stack software engineer`.
3. **Attention proposition** — `Engineering the whole problem, not just the code.`
4. **Proof rail** — tenure → scope → consequence.
5. **Continuation action** — `See the work ↓`.

Nothing else is admitted without demonstrating a distinct five-second job.

## `Full-stack` is load-bearing

Keep `Full-stack software engineer` in the opening identity line.

Here it communicates breadth across the technical system: frontend, backend/application architecture, data/persistence and the surrounding delivery stack rather than confinement to one technical layer. That job is distinct from the headline.

`Engineering the whole problem, not just the code.` communicates breadth across the engineering responsibility itself: problem/design, implementation, release, support, operation and consequence.

The two claims are complementary rather than duplicative:

- `Full-stack` answers **where in the technical system can Harley work?**
- the headline answers **where does Harley believe engineering responsibility stops?**

## Locked proof rail

The right-hand proof rail is not a miniature case study. It exists to make three facts scannable before the reader decides whether to read prose in depth.

Use this content hierarchy:

**5 years at The Access Group**

**Engineering responsibility for Access Checks, end to end.**  
Technical design → delivery → release → support → operation.

**Recently designed and delivered the service behind 2 additional paid screening checks.**

**See the work ↓**

The intended five-second extraction is:

`established engineer → consequential end-to-end engineering scope → commercial delivery`

The numeral `2` and the word `paid` are deliberate scan-level information. The rail should remain one quiet editorial object rather than becoming cards, badges, icons, a metric dashboard or an achievement list.

## Removed material

Retire the previous paragraph treatment that explained the Access Checks service in sentence form.

In particular, do not use `no source evidence, no successful result` in the first fold. That was a valuable commercial/reliability rule for the specific screening-agents delivery, but it is coupled to that project's value story and is not Harley's general engineering rule. It belongs with the deeper evidence for that work rather than in the universal five-second opening.

Do not add technology names, extra achievements, seniority adjectives, generic impact language or another professional claim to compensate for the shorter rail.

## Front-index decision

Remove the first-fold homepage jump/index links entirely.

The masthead already owns permanent navigation. A second navigation/index system in the same fold duplicates that job and consumes attention before the reader has any reason to care about labels such as Marketplace, Wild Bunch, Writing or Patch.

Do **not** relocate the index elsewhere merely to preserve it. The authored scroll and the movement headings provide orientation after the reader has chosen to continue.

`See the work ↓` is the single first-fold continuation action into the homepage composition.

## Visual temperament

The opening remains deliberately quiet. Attention should come from a strong proposition plus unusually concrete professional proof, not from spectacle.

The intended reaction is approximately:

> This person has a point of view, enough professional weight to support it, and receipts immediately below.

The fold should not compete visually with the later Marketplace, Wild Bunch, Writing or Patch movements. Its restraint establishes the professional frame that allows those later changes of register to read as authored evidence rather than portfolio theatre.

## Review boundary

**Fold one is locked.**

Wireframe/source work may now remove the front index and replace the existing proof paragraph with the locked proof-rail hierarchy. Optical spacing and responsive composition may still be tuned around that content, but the fold's jobs, admitted elements and copy hierarchy should not be reopened unless rendered integration exposes a concrete contradiction.
