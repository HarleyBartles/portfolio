# Phase 8P visual-language checkpoint

**Status:** Durable PR #46 checkpoint after substrate, colour-authority and spatial-language discovery. Phase 8P remains active. Public non-home implementation planning and homepage Phase 8 remain blocked.

This file preserves the visual-language decisions and discoveries made after the merged typography checkpoint so they survive the Cloud design room. It is subordinate to the wider [`phase-8-cloud-decision-record.md`](./phase-8-cloud-decision-record.md) and the settled [`phase-8p-typography-contract.md`](./phase-8p-typography-contract.md), but it is the current baton for the remaining Phase 8P discovery.

## Governing direction

The leading visual territory is **Working Engineering Record**: serious engineering, visibly handled by a human.

The system should feel authored through revision, judgement, evidence and custody rather than through decorative signs of hand-making. Humanity belongs in the way material is corrected, annotated, selected, challenged and handed off.

`Independent Technical Publication` remains a useful challenger/reference territory rather than a rejected direction. `Case File` is rejected.

The site must not answer the 2026 AI-design convergence problem by mechanically inverting its tells. Beige/cream, rusty orange, huge serif display, tracked subheads, ticker bars and rounded outlined component-library sameness were useful warning signals, not a list whose opposites automatically produce authorship. Faux-handmade anti-AI styling can converge just as easily.

Every conspicuous house-style choice should ideally have a provenance story: it should be explainable from Harley's actual material, project structure, evidence or editorial job rather than from generic visual-fashion preference.

## Settled typography input

Typography is already settled by [`phase-8p-typography-contract.md`](./phase-8p-typography-contract.md) and is not reopened here.

- Source Sans 3 is the shared site voice and structural/display family outside authored longform.
- Source Serif 4 is the semantic authored-longform register only.
- Source Code Pro is genuine technical content only.
- Serif is not a prestige accent; Mono is not engineering decoration.

The deterministic typography specimen proves typography only and must not be treated as a production composition.

## Settled substrate

**Shared light substrate: cool mineral `#E6EAEB`.**

The substrate is neutral ground, not a brand colour. It has enough material presence to avoid generic white-SaaS blankness without reading as cream/paper or as blue-grey software-product atmosphere.

Rejected alternatives from the deterministic substrate comparison:

- cool near-white `#F7F8F8`: too blank; project colour and dark evidence surfaces had to carry nearly all identity;
- restrained blue-grey `#E8EEF2`: too narratively active; it pushed the shell toward software/dev-tool territory and made Patch teal harmonise with the shell rather than arrive as project-owned colour.

Default site mode remains light. Default-dark remains rejected as unearned cyber/dev-tool shorthand; optional user-selected dark mode may be considered later if the implementation can support it without changing the visual contract.

## Settled project-colour authority

Project identity comes from **project-native colour**, not a universal house accent.

**Selected behaviour: bounded bleed.** Project-native colour may leave its source artifact and act on a restrained, finite area tied to a meaningful section, edge or relationship. The field must visibly terminate and return to the cool-mineral substrate. The shared substrate remains the dominant ground.

`Bounded` describes authority, not a mandatory rectangle/component. The shape and location of a bleed must come from the material and composition rather than from a repeated decorative recipe.

Rejected alternatives:

- **Local only:** too contained; project material looked inserted into a neutral host and the surrounding composition did not feel related to the work.
- **Route atmosphere:** too sovereign; project colour became the route theme and weakened the sense that one authored portfolio system holds different bodies of work.

Patch supplies a governed teal family led by canonical `#17B1A6`; the portfolio may borrow its controlled colour behaviour, calm competence, bounded workflow, readable hierarchy and processual modularity. It must not globally borrow mascot geometry, rounded-card language, cartoon shading, teal title bars or literal reference-sheet UI. Patch character imagery is not a general portfolio asset and should not be invented or approximated in Phase 8P surfaces.

Wild Bunch supplies earth colour, spatial modularity and constructed-world structure, not a Western theme. The current Dustwell town image is real project material. The current yellow marker is a development artifact/future player sprite and olive blocks are development placeholders/future trail-head tile; neither is an earned palette source. Stable visual material is the dust/ochre terrain, darker road/taupe, dark interface chrome/off-white text and the intentional tiled town-hub field.

## Settled spatial grammar

**The rectilinear grid is the grammar. Alignment is the default. Evidence may break or reorganise one local grid relationship when the content earns the interruption.**

Selected baseline: **earned interruption**.

The default page should be disciplined and aligned. A project-owned artifact may cross a column boundary, rule or local relationship when its visual or semantic weight justifies doing so. The interruption must remain bounded, legible and rare. It is not a requirement to add one quirky breakout to every section.

Rejected as the global default:

- **Aligned ledger:** useful discipline but too institutional when alignment itself becomes the personality.
- **Rail + field as route skeleton:** too much template pressure; unlike evidence was forced into the same narrow-context/broad-evidence composition.

### Rail + field survives as an earned special case

Rail + field is retained in the vocabulary rather than discarded.

A local rail may be introduced when a narrow, semantically distinct stream of context genuinely benefits from being held alongside a broader evidence field: for example classification, state, constraints, chronology or custody. It belongs only to the content span it serves, not automatically to the route.

Guards:

- removing the rail should cause a real information-architecture loss, not merely make the composition less interesting;
- the rail must not become branded side furniture or a coloured stripe used for identity;
- it must not steal width the evidence needs;
- at narrow widths it collapses into normal semantic reading order rather than preserving desktop asymmetry at all costs.

## Human intervention / anti-gimmick boundary

The working record may show human judgement, but interior routes should not perform humanity through decorative disorder.

- Faux handwriting, scribbled margins, tape, scrapbook collage, torn-paper tricks and fake corrections are not part of the interior system.
- Human intervention should be extremely sparse: likely two to four genuinely earned moments across the entire non-home site, with most routes containing none.
- If an intervention can be anticipated as a slot in the template, it has become a gimmick.
- Plain technical correction can work when it is content, for example a real rename/diff plus `// call it what it is`.
- The later homepage may have more expressive freedom; interior routes establish the disciplined grammar first.

Principle: **inside the book, humanity appears as revision and judgement; on the cover, humanity may appear as expression.**

## Imagery discoveries already settled

Project-native imagery is allowed to do identity work. There is no universal dark evidence-card aesthetic and no requirement to force unlike media into identical frames.

Wild Bunch owns a sparse image-edge treatment: a soft, irregular tintype/old photographic plate/emulsion density-loss edge. Adjacent text or metadata may intrude slightly into the low-information faded zone. The fade itself is approved as a useful primitive; earlier generated imagery failed because the image did not itself read as tintype, making the fade look attached rather than inherent.

The Wild Bunch edge treatment must not become a clean CSS linear/radial gradient, perfect feather, torn-paper edge, burnt-map effect, fake sepia layer, scrapbook device or general site treatment. The surrounding portfolio remains modern and neutral; the historic association comes from Wild Bunch material.

The intentional tiled Dustwell town-hub field may contribute restrained repeated units, stepped alignment, modular crops or field rhythm where relevant. The portfolio must not become a literal game board.

## Research-derived pressure on the system

The current direction has been checked against external premium/editorial references and current AI-design convergence rather than derived from taste alone.

Useful lessons retained from that research:

- National Academies / AREA17: conspicuous house choices can be sourced from the institution's own visual material rather than invented as generic brand styling.
- Simons Foundation: a restrained shell can let subject material supply colour.
- Menil / AREA17: spatial language, asymmetry and material can control atmosphere without a loud house palette.
- Harvard University Press / AREA17: a light reading environment need not be cream/paper.
- Perfectly Imperfect: specific sourced identity choices are more defensible than generic retro styling; the transferable lesson is source -> choice, not appearance-copy.
- The Baffler / Pentagram: coherence can organise substantial variation.
- Lloyd's annual report / Pentagram: utilitarian, documentary composition can be premium when the information structure itself does the design work.

The current system should therefore be judged by whether an informed reader can explain its conspicuous choices from this portfolio's own material and editorial needs, not by whether it resembles any one reference.

## Discovery stop rule

Phase 8P now has an explicit diminishing-returns boundary.

A design decision is ready for durable recording once it has:

1. been rendered rather than argued only in prose;
2. been tested against materially different content or pressure cases;
3. survived Harley's taste decision;
4. survived one deliberate challenge to the selected direction; and
5. left no unresolved alternative that would materially change how local Sol composes the route.

Once those conditions are met, the decision is closed unless a later integrated proof exposes a concrete contradiction. New references or minor parameter preferences are not enough reason to reopen it.

Discovery has stopped paying rent when new work changes parameter values or edge-case styling but no longer changes the visual vocabulary, authority boundaries, route composition or implementation decisions available to local Sol. At that point the work belongs in implementation and rendered refinement rather than Cloud discovery.

The final contract should synthesise already-recorded decisions, not become the first durable record of them.

## Remaining finite discovery plan

There are **four remaining Phase 8P stages** before the design-room baton can become `Ready for local implementation planning`.

### 1. Evidence and imagery custody

Settle how real receipts enter the page across materially different evidence families: screenshots/project imagery, code/diffs, diagrams/documents and related captions/provenance.

Questions to settle:

- when evidence sits directly in the grid versus when it requires framing;
- whether borders/radii exist and what semantic job they perform;
- caption/provenance relationship to the artifact;
- how project-specific treatments such as the Wild Bunch fade coexist with the shared system;
- how to avoid a universal dark developer-card or universal framed-component aesthetic.

Stop when local Sol can place representative evidence correctly without needing a bespoke art-direction decision for every file type. Do not design every possible MIME type.

### 2. Page rhythm and density

Settle broad spacing behaviour rather than pixel-tuning the implementation.

Questions to settle:

- default breathing room between major sections;
- how rules and negative space establish hierarchy;
- when evidence-dense passages may deliberately tighten;
- how longform reading rhythm differs from evidence-heavy project rhythm without becoming a second design system.

Stop once the relationships are clear enough to compose routes consistently. Exact `44px` versus `48px` tuning is local implementation work unless it changes the perceived character of the system.

### 3. Shared shell and navigation

Settle the visual/semantic contract for the masthead, route context and ordinary interior navigation while preserving the existing navigation behaviour contract in the wider decision record.

Questions to settle:

- how the shared shell remains recognisable without becoming the site's visual protagonist;
- how Projects, Writing, Patch, About and CV sit in the shell;
- how route context is shown without tracked-label/ticker/chip regression;
- how active/current state is legible;
- how shell geometry behaves at narrow widths.

Do not spend discovery time on every hover easing or implementation animation.

### 4. Integrated route proof

Assemble the accepted system into representative non-home compositions using real material rather than continuing to test isolated primitives.

Minimum route families:

1. a project/evidence-heavy route;
2. an authored article route;
3. a professional/heterogeneous route such as About.

Patch becomes an additional proof only if it exposes a real problem those three cannot expose; it is not automatically a fourth composition exercise.

Proof each representative composition at:

- desktop;
- narrow/mobile width;
- 200% zoom;
- keyboard/focus where the shell or evidence interaction requires it;
- contrast and missing-media intent where relevant.

There is at most **one substantive correction loop** after the integrated proof. A correction loop is justified when the integration falsifies a rule or exposes a contradiction, for example bounded bleed damaging article hierarchy or an earned interruption collapsing semantic order at narrow width. Minor spacing/alpha preferences do not reopen discovery.

## Exit criterion

Phase 8P discovery is complete when:

- the four remaining stages above are closed;
- the integrated proof demonstrates one coherent system across materially different route families;
- desktop, narrow and 200% conditions do not expose a visual-language contradiction;
- the design still passes the 50% AI-convergence valuation gate;
- Harley accepts the complete visual contract;
- remaining open questions are implementation/refinement questions rather than art-direction questions.

Only then may the baton become **`Ready for local implementation planning`**.

Local Sol may then write the JIT implementation plan and solve engineering seams inside the accepted contract. It may not invent or materially redirect the visual language.

Homepage Phase 8 remains blocked until Phase 8P is complete, the non-home remediation is implemented and rendered, its evidence passes the valuation gate, and Harley accepts the result.
