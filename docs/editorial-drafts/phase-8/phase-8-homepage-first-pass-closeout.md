# Phase 8 homepage — first-pass editorial closeout

**Status:** First-pass homepage editorial/art-direction review closed on 1 September 2026. The page now has a coherent fold-by-fold contract and is ready for one updated integrated wireframe pass on this same branch and draft PR.

This file is a forward closeout record. It does not rewrite the earlier wireframe, rejected assets, side-quest history or decision trail.

## What has been decided

The homepage is no longer an open-ended design discovery exercise. Its current sequence and reader-time contract are settled enough to implement in the disposable integrated wireframe:

1. **Fold 1 — professional opening:** approximately **5 seconds** to earn attention.
2. **Fold 2 — Agent Asset Marketplace / Superpowers+:** the next approximately **10 seconds** to turn attention into interest.
3. **Fold 3 — Wild Bunch:** approximately **15 seconds** to prove that a roughly five-minute interior route will repay the reader's time.
4. **Fold 4 — Writing:** another independent approximately **15-second bid** for a five-minute interior commitment, especially for readers who did not care about Fold 3.
5. **Fold 5 — Patch / The Usual Specialists:** another independent approximately **15-second bid**, using the already accepted image-led movement as the final visual crest.
6. **Professional close:** not another proof fold. It is the quiet reciprocal invitation after the page has made its case.

From Fold 3 onward, a movement must work as a fresh recovery point. A visitor is allowed to ignore one subject and be won back by the next. The page may accumulate trust, but no later fold may require careful reading of the previous one to make sense.

The overall promise is therefore:

> approximately thirty seconds of homepage attention should earn enough trust that at least one deeper five-minute route feels worth the reader's time.

## Page rhythm

The accepted macro rhythm is:

**quiet professional opening → visual/agentic opening-up → calmer warm technical receipt → austere authored writing → Patch spectacle → quiet reciprocal close**

The Phase 8P visual language remains the shared vocabulary. Homepage distinctiveness comes from orchestration, scale, sequencing, evidence placement, project-native colour and authored responsive composition — not from inventing a second site-wide typography, palette or component system.

### Professional close

The previous close was structurally good but too deferential in copy. The accepted reciprocal close is:

**Heading**

`I've shown you how I work.`

**Body**

`If that looks like the kind of engineering you want on your team, I'd like to hear what you're building.`

**Primary route**

`Tell me about it →`

**Secondary routes**

`Read my CV`  
`About me`

The primary route is the contact action. The close should feel like `I've shown you mine; show me yours` without hostility, swagger or sales-funnel language. It is an invitation to a reciprocal professional conversation.

Keep the quiet decompression after Patch. Do not add another visual flourish, summary, testimonial, final manifesto or generic `Interested in working together?` hero.

## Release decision for Fold 2

The homepage is **not automatically blocked** until the fully developed Fold 2 visual system exists.

The next wireframe should prove the smallest honest edition of the accepted Fold 2 language. If that smaller edition looks deliberate and clears the ten-second job beside the stronger Wild Bunch, Writing and Patch movements, it is eligible to move forward with the rest of the homepage. The richer Fold 2 system can then become a later refinement of a shipped visual language rather than a prerequisite for discovering it.

If the smaller edition reads as unfinished, generic or visibly below the page's quality bar, publication remains blocked on Fold 2. That decision belongs after the next integrated rendered proof, not before it.
