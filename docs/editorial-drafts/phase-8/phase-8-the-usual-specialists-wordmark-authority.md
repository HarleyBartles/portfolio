## Accepted object

The wordmark is one fixed `1120 × 240` proportional object. Wide, intermediate and narrow uses must scale the complete object uniformly. Its parts must not reflow, resize independently or be reconstructed as responsive text.

The accepted geometry is recorded by [`phase-8-the-usual-specialists-wordmark-refinement-proof.html`](./phase-8-the-usual-specialists-wordmark-refinement-proof.html):

- `THE USUAL` is Source Sans 3 at weight `900`;
- `SPECIALISTS` is upright Chassis Black;
- the complete `THE` word and its two rules occupy exactly the same rendered height as `USUAL`;
- the visible left edge of `THE` aligns with the visible left edge of `SPECIALISTS`;
- `THE USUAL` reads as one phrase with an ordinary optical word-space;
- the complete upper phrase scales proportionally from the shared left anchor until its right edge meets the end of the `E` in `SPE`; and
- the lower word, upper phrase, rules and whitespace retain the accepted `1120 × 240` relationship at every rendered size.

The historical A/B/C comparison remains selection evidence only. A / Balanced datum is the accepted parent; the refinement proof above is the final geometry authority.
