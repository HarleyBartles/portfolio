# Phase 8 Lawful Heist title-font proof brief

**Status:** Retired on 31 August 2026. `Lawful Heist` is no longer the public title, so this candidate comparison must not be treated as current title direction. The replacement proof is [`phase-8-the-usual-specialists-wordmark-proof.html`](./phase-8-the-usual-specialists-wordmark-proof.html).

## Purpose

`Lawful Heist` is being presented with deliberate comic-cover syntax. The title should therefore not inherit the ordinary site sans by default. The adventure earns one deliberately selected display face for its title lockup while the surrounding site/interface copy remains in the shared portfolio voice.

This is a bounded exception, not a new site-wide typography system.

## Candidates to proof

Create one browser-reviewable inspection surface showing the same `Lawful Heist` lockup in these three treatments:

1. **Astounder Squared BB Bold** — leading candidate.
2. **Astounder Squared BB Regular** — tests whether the face itself supplies enough cover authority without the extra mass of Bold.
3. **Changa One** — open-licence control with a similarly dense display role but a different voice.

Do not add more candidates in this pass unless one of the three cannot be rendered lawfully/faithfully. The purpose is selection, not a type catalogue.

## Inspection surface

Create a small isolated proof page beside the Heist homepage proof. It should let Harley/Cloud judge the fonts in the actual job rather than in a specimen sheet.

Show all three treatments under equivalent conditions:

- exact text: `Lawful Heist`;
- same foreground/background colours;
- same approximate title footprint and line break (`Lawful` / `Heist`) unless a face makes that visibly impossible;
- same supporting `Adventures of Patch`, `The crew is in.` and `Meet the crew` context in Source Sans 3;
- no outline, bevel, shadow, texture, skew or other title effect in the first comparison;
- tune only font size/line-height/letter-spacing enough to give each face a fair equivalent lockup rather than forcing identical CSS metrics;
- show at least one wide and one narrow/mobile context, because density and line shape may reverse the preference at small widths.

Prefer a simple side-by-side/stacked comparison surface over rebuilding the full Heist spread three times if that makes comparison clearer. A second section may place the candidates into a cropped approximation of the real dark title field if useful.

## Selection criteria

Judge the title face on:

- **comic-cover authority:** reads as intentionally lettered/title-like, not ordinary interface typography;
- **Heist fit:** energetic and authored without sliding into military stencil, noir parody, superhero sound-effect lettering or children's-comic novelty;
- **shape at two lines:** `Lawful` over `Heist` should form a strong title mass with useful unevenness/character;
- **legibility:** immediate at desktop and narrow mobile sizes;
- **compatibility:** can coexist with the warm Heist artwork, paper carriers and red K without becoming another competing effect;
- **restraint:** the type itself should do the work before any decorative treatment is considered.

The desired reaction is `this is the title lettering for this comic`, not `the website has switched fonts`.

## Licensing/custody boundary

Do not commit or redistribute font binaries unless repository use is clearly licensed for that font.

For the proof, use a lawful available source or locally available licensed font. If Astounder Squared BB cannot be rendered lawfully in the local proof environment, do not substitute a lookalike and label it Astounder. Record the access/licensing block explicitly and still produce the comparison surface for the candidates that can be rendered accurately.

Any production adoption must include explicit font/source/licence custody appropriate to web use.

## Handoff

Return with:

- the inspection-surface path/URL;
- which candidate/weight is actually rendering for each treatment;
- any font-access/licensing limitation encountered;
- one screenshot-equivalent wide state and one narrow state available for Cloud/Harley review;
- no recommendation presented as settled taste. Harley/Cloud will choose from the rendered proof.

The earlier Astounder/Changa comparison was superseded before public integration. Its uncommitted browser artifact was removed rather than preserving the retired `Lawful Heist` treatment as an active option. No candidate font from that proof was integrated into production.
