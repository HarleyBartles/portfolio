# Phase 8 homepage — Fold 4 Writing lock

**Status:** Accepted rendered-proof direction on 1 September 2026. The current Writing movement passes for Phase 8 homepage design purposes. Do not open a typography/composition side quest merely to polish this proof specimen.

## Timing contract

Fold 4 inherits the post-Fold-2 homepage contract: it gets approximately **15 seconds to earn a roughly five-minute interior commitment**.

This bid must work independently. A reader may have bounced off Fold 3 at `event sourcing`, `architecture` or simply `too technical`; one more scroll should present a materially different reason to trust that five minutes spent inside the site will be worthwhile.

Later folds may accumulate trust from readers who follow the whole page, but they must also work as recovery points for readers who did not care about the previous subject.

## Fifteen-second promise

Writing wins the commitment through a recognisably human, self-critical story rather than another technical receipt.

For the current proof specimen, the promise is immediately legible from the title and précis:

- Harley made agentic engineering harder than it needed to be;
- the machinery grew until returning to the work meant accepting a cleanup project first;
- the correction was not `less process` by reflex, but keeping the real boundaries and making every surviving surface justify its presence.

The fold does not need to summarise the article. It needs to make the reader trust that the longer piece contains a real lived mistake, a non-trivial correction and a useful conclusion.

## Accepted composition

**Typography is the visual.** No illustrative substitute is required.

The rendered proof succeeds because:

- the article title is the dominant visual object;
- the précis occupies a separate editorial plane rather than behaving like card copy under a heading;
- large negative space is active composition, not missing content;
- the route remains quiet interface copy;
- the movement changes register clearly after the warmer, evidence-led Wild Bunch fold;
- the result reads as a publication surface rather than another portfolio feature component.

On wider layouts, the title and précis may occupy offset planes with substantial open space between them. The relationship should remain deliberate, but the solution is not to fill the field with furniture.

On narrow layouts, the title may become almost poster-scale and the précis may naturally occupy more vertical depth. This is acceptable when the composition remains readable and austere rather than collapsing into a generic blog card.

## Edition-specific authorship

The current representative article is:

`I made agentic engineering harder than it needed to be`

This is deliberately an awkwardly long title and therefore a useful pressure-test specimen. The fact that the current proof survives it is evidence for the composition system; it is **not** a requirement that every Writing edition use identical line breaks, scale, précis length or spatial proportions.

Each selected article receives an **authored homepage précis**. The invariant is the editorial relationship, not a fixed word count or card geometry:

- title dominates;
- précis is a distinct second plane;
- negative space remains meaningful;
- route action stays subordinate;
- title length and précis density are allowed to materially reshape the composition inside the governed responsive grid.

Do not mechanically truncate every précis to make all editions look alike. Equally, do not allow an edition-specific précis to turn the homepage into the article itself; the 15-second contract still governs.

## Current proof copy

Representative title:

`I made agentic engineering harder than it needed to be`

Current authored précis:

`I built an agent organisation around a novel, then filled the repository with roughly 300 agent-facing documents until returning to the work meant accepting a cleanup project first. I kept the real boundaries and removed the theatre, with one question for every surviving surface: why are you here?`

The current route label, `Read the article →`, is sufficient for this proof and does not block the movement. Its exact final wording may be revisited later if the page-level invitation language benefits from a more authored route, but that is not a Fold 4 design blocker and does not justify a side quest now.

## Anti-directions

Do not add a thumbnail, illustration, decorative image, card shell, `featured` badge, feed metadata, drop shadow or other furniture merely because the movement is visually austere.

Do not normalise all Writing editions into one rigid component geometry.

Do not mistake empty space for unfinished design and fill it to make the fold busier.

Do not spend the five-minute article on the homepage in an attempt to strengthen the fifteen-second promise.

## Acceptance result

The current rendered wide, intermediate and narrow proofs pass the design-room test, including the deliberately difficult long-title specimen.

Fold 4 is therefore settled enough to park. Later implementation may tune optical spacing, responsive type scale and edition-specific copy, but should preserve the accepted editorial hierarchy and typography-first art direction unless rendered production evidence exposes a concrete contradiction.
