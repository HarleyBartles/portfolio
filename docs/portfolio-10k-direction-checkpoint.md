## The outcome

The portfolio exists to help Harley land a senior full-stack engineering role.
It should present him confidently as:

> A senior full-stack engineer working at the frontier of agentic engineering.

This is a statement about level, direction, and the work the portfolio will
prove. It is not a substitute for evidence, and it must not overwrite the
formal job title attached to an employment record.

The target is a site that feels as if a thoughtful agency charged £10,000 to
understand its subject, find the strongest story, art-direct the evidence, and
finish every public surface. More visual decoration will not achieve that. The
remaining gain must come from sharper professional positioning, deeper proof,
project-native imagery, stronger editorial decisions, and the removal of
unfinished states.

## Audience order

1. **Hiring managers and interviewers** need to identify Harley's level,
   relevance, and strongest evidence quickly.
2. **Senior engineers and technical peers** need enough substance to inspect
   decisions, trade-offs, and execution.
3. **Agentic-engineering practitioners and learners** may return for the
   writing, projects, learning material, and Patch stories.

The homepage must make the professional proposition legible without turning
every page into a pitch. Projects, articles, and artefacts should remain useful
in their own right. About is the deliberate place where the subtext becomes
explicit: assess Harley for a role, understand his experience and working
style, download a CV, and make contact safely.

## What “senior” means here

The differentiator is not a claim to know every stack and it is not domain-
driven design by itself. DDD is one tool in a larger engineering bag.

The senior claim rests on judgement:

- identifying when a problem genuinely earns complex architecture;
- executing that architecture well when it does;
- resisting complexity when a simpler system is the responsible choice;
- making trade-offs and uncertainty explicit;
- learning unfamiliar technology when the problem requires it; and
- leaving inspectable evidence instead of asking a reader to trust adjectives.

Wild Bunch can prove that Harley understands and can execute a sophisticated
domain model when the problem warrants it. This portfolio can prove the
complementary judgement: a static publishing site does not earn a runtime
backend, a DDD architecture, or a distributed system. The ability to tell those
problems apart is more important than making DDD the personal brand.

Harley does not want to perform universal proficiency. If he does not know
something, he will say so and learn it when needed. Where he does make a claim,
the portfolio should show the work behind it.

## The evidence model

The professional case has three foundations.

### 1. Enterprise practice

Harley has worked continuously as a professional software developer since
6 February 2019. At this checkpoint that is approximately seven and a half
years, not the 6.5 years currently shown on About. The transition into Access
had no employment gap. Future chronology work must preserve that continuous
record rather than inferring a gap from month-only LinkedIn dates.

Enterprise work establishes duration, delivery context, responsibility, and
exposure to systems that must survive beyond a demo. Some of the strongest
detail is proprietary. The public site can discuss roles, responsibilities,
technologies, problem classes, and lessons at an appropriate level. It must not
publish internal architecture, processes, customer information, screenshots,
private metrics, or other Access material merely to make a case study look
more impressive. More detail can be offered in an interview where appropriate.

Professional discretion should read as normal senior judgement, not as coy
“secret work” marketing.

### 2. Disciplined independent work

The personal projects are hobbies in the sense that Harley chooses to do them;
they are not casual in their execution. Each exists because it satisfies part
of his interest in agentic engineering, and each should prove a distinct part
of how he operates.

| Evidence | What it should prove |
| --- | --- |
| Wild Bunch | Complex domain modelling, DDD, deterministic behaviour, and architectural depth when the domain earns it. |
| This portfolio | Proportionate architecture, static delivery, quality gates, accessibility, source custody, and resistance to unnecessary backend complexity. |
| Agent Asset Marketplace | Governance, packaging, provenance, repeatability, validation, and durable agent infrastructure. |
| Agentic Learning Lab | Leadership, teaching, safe delegation, and making agentic practice usable by people who are not software engineers. |
| Adventures of Patch | A controlled creative pipeline, reusable production assets, and memorable technical communication. |
| Technical writing | Clarity of thought and the ability to make engineering decisions legible to other people. |

The project pages must turn this map into inspectable evidence. A repository
link, a short description, and a status badge are useful, but they are not yet
a senior-level case study.

### 3. Formal AI engineering study

Harley began QA's **AI Engineer Level 6 Apprenticeship** in February 2026 and

>

> I did not take the traditional university route. I built my engineering

## What “frontier of agentic engineering” must mean

This phrase is a useful and ambitious position only if the site gives it
content. Here it spans three connected forms of practice:

1. building reliable agent-driven development systems and workflows;
2. using agentic methods to build and improve products; and
3. developing deeper AI and machine-learning foundations through the Level 6
   programme.

The current public evidence is strongest in the first category. Professional
practice contributes to the second within confidentiality limits, and formal
study is broadening the third. The site must not imply foundation-model
research, proprietary Access knowledge, or expertise that the available proof
does not support.

About is where the editorial illusion deliberately breaks and the hiring case
becomes direct. It should eventually contain:

1. a concise current professional thesis;
2. the public-safe Access practice;
3. the continuous Barbican-to-Arch foundation;
4. the earlier Brand Addition progression and commercial context;

6. the independent-project evidence map;
7. a working style built around proportionate architecture, honest unknowns,
   and evidence before claims;

The page should not become a LinkedIn replica. It should select the facts that

form is the professional default. Its delivery state must be truthful and its

## Provisional £10k workstreams

The following inventory is deliberately broader than a single implementation
plan. Sequence and slice boundaries still need human approval.

1. **Professional truth and content model.** Replace stale duration literals,
   decide whether to derive experience from start dates or say “since February
   2019,” and establish one fact source for About and CV.
2. **About, CV, and contact.** Write the authored career narrative, surface the
   Level 6 signal accurately, produce the real CV, and connect the form.
3. **Flagship case studies.** Turn selected projects into stories of problem,
   responsibility, constraints, decisions, current evidence, and learning.
4. **Project-native visual evidence.** Add Wild Bunch captures, a Patch pipeline
   composition, Marketplace system visuals, and stronger Learning Lab proof.
5. **Homepage choreography.** Recompose the homepage around the stronger
   evidence, remove adjacent duplication, and ensure the professional thesis
   is immediately clear without adding sales-page clutter.
6. **Flagship writing.** Deeply groom two existing essays, add diagrams or pull
   quotes where they earn their place, and later add one strong full-stack,
   product, recovery, or Wild Bunch decision piece to broaden the archive.
7. **Publishing finish.** Add the custom domain, per-route social artwork and
   metadata, RSS, privacy-conscious analytics, and a stable direct-route first
   paint.
8. **One interactive proof artefact.** Consider a Marketplace explorer, Patch
   pipeline visualisation, Learning Lab exercise, or Wild Bunch replay/event
   viewer. Choose one only when it demonstrates engineering better than a
   static case study.
9. **Final editorial and visual audit.** Remove placeholders and dead ends,
   review the whole journey at the repository's required viewports and access
   modes, and test whether the hiring argument survives close technical
   inspection.

A likely high-value opening is to develop About/professional truth and the
first flagship case study together: one states the senior proposition and one
proves it. That is a recommendation, not yet the approved epic order.

## What £10k quality will mean

The final definition of done should be made measurable in the later spec and
roadmap. The intended quality bar is already clear:

- within seconds, a visitor can identify the target role, agentic focus, and a
  reason to believe the claim;
- every strong claim leads to evidence, a concrete example, or an honest
  boundary;
- each flagship project has an authored visual and editorial treatment suited
  to its specific story;
- no public route contains stale facts, placeholders, broken contact states,
  confusing loading flashes, or misleading maturity claims;
- motion communicates hierarchy or state and respects reduced motion;
- long-form reading, mobile layouts, keyboard use, zoom, metadata, previews,
  and direct links feel intentionally finished; and
- the architecture stays proportional to the actual site rather than using
  complexity as theatre.

## Explicit anti-goals

- Do not brand Harley as “the DDD engineer.”
- Do not claim universal expertise or hide honest unknowns.
- Do not imply that the apprenticeship awards a bachelor's degree.
- Do not publish proprietary employer material or manufacture unverifiable
  impact metrics.
- Do not turn the site into a tech-logo wall, generic résumé template, or
  repeated “hire me” pitch.
- Do not add stock imagery, a generic AI hero, decorative autoplay, or a
  conventional carousel.
- Do not add a runtime backend, authentication, search, taxonomy, or other
  machinery until a concrete need earns it.
- Do not make Patch, Wild Bunch, or any single technique the whole identity.
- Do not chase article volume or interactive novelty at the expense of the
  strongest evidence already available.
