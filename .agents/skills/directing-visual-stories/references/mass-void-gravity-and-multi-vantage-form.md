# Mass, void, gravity, and multi-vantage form

Use this route when an object, character, monument, prop, or spatial form must tell
its story in the round. Sculpture contributes a composition that changes as the
audience moves: mass and empty space, balance and instability, surface and shadow,
body and site.

## Compose form and void

Name the solid masses and the voids they shape. A gap can become a passage, wound,
frame, withheld centre, or place for another body. Negative space is not leftover
background; inspect its silhouette from every important viewpoint.

Use weight and support causally:

- grounded mass can imply endurance, authority, immobility, or burden;
- suspension can imply delicacy, threat, release, or dependence;
- cantilever and imbalance can hold anticipation;
- visible joints, props, seams, and counterweights can expose how survival is
  maintained;
- a changing shadow can act as a second, time-dependent composition.

Material behaviour matters. Stone compresses and chips; cloth sags and folds;
metal bends, reflects, rusts, or rings; timber spans, splinters, and bears grain.
Direct the force carried by the material rather than asking only for its appearance.

## Direct multiple viewpoints

Identify approach, first silhouette, privileged or revealing view, side/rear
discovery, and departure memory. Decide what becomes visible only by moving and
what must remain recognisable from all likely views. A form designed for one hero
angle is not complete when it will rotate, scroll in parallax, occupy a navigable
world, or be seen from several responsive crops.

Scale is bodily. State whether the viewer could hold, touch, lean against, pass
through, look up to, or be enclosed by the form. Human scale, monumental scale,
miniature scale, and uncertain scale produce different relationships even when the
object silhouette is unchanged.

Kinetic form adds a state space rather than one pose. Record range, balance,
trigger, tempo, collision risk, shadow behaviour, and the configurations that carry
the story. Movement should reveal a relationship, not merely prove that the object
can animate.

### Route output

Record: dominant masses and shaped voids; support and gravity logic; material force;
approach and multi-vantage sequence; bodily scale; changing shadow or reflection;
kinetic range if present; relation to site; and the silhouette/void invariant that
must survive translation.

### Avoid

- evaluating a three-dimensional form from one attractive render;
- treating empty space as transparent padding;
- applying a material skin without its weight, joint, wear, or structural logic;
- using monumental scale without deciding the viewer relationship it creates;
- animating every component instead of reserving movement as evidence.
