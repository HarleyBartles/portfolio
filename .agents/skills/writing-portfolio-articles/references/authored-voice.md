# Authored Voice

Voice comes from perspective, selection, specificity, judgement and rhythm. Slang, swearing, fragments and jokes may contribute; none substitutes for having something particular to say.

The authorised register permits directness, contractions, technical specificity, self-implication, honest rough edges and dry humour. It does not manufacture intimacy, certainty, rebellion or cleverness. Public prose does not use em dashes.

When prose feels generic, ask:

- Who made the decision?
- What concrete mechanism or consequence is being hidden by abstraction?
- What does the author actually judge here?
- Is the sentence explaining an insight the story has already proved?
- Would the joke survive without signalling that it is a joke?
- Is informality doing work or merely performing personality?

Preserve supplied language when it is stronger than the edit. Leave consequential voice uncertainty for Harley rather than averaging it into smooth professional prose.

Profanity is editorial vocabulary, not heat. Consult doctrine for the whole-site 12A standard and judge meaning, tone, repetition and cumulative effect before retaining it.
