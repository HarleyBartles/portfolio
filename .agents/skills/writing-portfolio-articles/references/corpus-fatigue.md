# Corpus and Fatigue

Use the live corpus to notice recurrence, never to reconstruct a voice model. Current articles are fluid editorial material, not golden examples, fixtures or phrase banks.

Look for repeated openings, section shapes, antitheses, transitions, metaphors, story families, pull quotes, question sequences and ending devices. Recurrence is a prompt for judgement. An owned preference may recur; an unexamined default may need interruption.

AI-fatigue watch classes include:

- homogenised cadence or uniform section architecture;
- empty antithesis, rhetorical triplets and symmetrical lists;
- abstraction where a mechanism belongs;
- generic scene-setting, uplift or emotional emphasis;
- excessive signposting, false intimacy or unearned certainty;
- conspicuous polish without individual judgement;
- explanatory tails after the point has landed;
- random roughening added merely to sound human;
- imitation of surface quirks from earlier articles.

Classify findings as preserve, observed, candidate, repair or abstain. The remedy is renewed authorship: recover proposition, evidence, decisions and consequences. Synonym swapping is not a voice edit.

The audit may identify exact repetitions and distributions. Read every finding in context. It cannot decide whether recurrence is alive, tired or deliberate.
