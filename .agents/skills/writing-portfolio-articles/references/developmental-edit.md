# Developmental Edit

Read the complete draft before changing it. State the article you actually found, not the one its title claims to be.

## Pass order

1. **Proposition:** can one sentence name the centre without flattening it?
2. **Promise:** does the opening establish the relationship the body fulfils?
3. **Form:** is the chosen form helping the material?
4. **Movement:** what changes in the reader's understanding section by section?
5. **Evidence:** which turns depend on fact, memory, inference or opinion?
6. **Counterpressure:** where would an intelligent sceptic resist?
7. **Relevance:** which good material belongs in another article?
8. **Repetition:** does recurrence deepen, or merely repeat?
9. **Ending:** does it feel caused by the article?

Produce a diagnosis before a rewrite. Name the strongest existing choices and the cost of disturbing them. Prioritise a small number of structural changes; do not hide uncertainty beneath a comprehensive-looking list.

After revision, compare whole drafts. A smoother local passage does not compensate for lost tension, specificity or causal movement.
