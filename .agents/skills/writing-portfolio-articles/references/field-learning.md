# Field Learning

The [field-notes document](../../../docs/article-writing-field-notes.md) is a non-binding evidence log. Consult it only when a current problem resembles a recorded candidate; current doctrine and the article in front of you remain authoritative.

## Accepted-article retrospective

Every accepted article or accepted preserve decision gets a short retrospective in its existing decision record or PR. State what the process helped reveal, what it hid or distorted, and whether the current workflow needs a change. Record the reason when the answer is no. This is a bounded editorial decision, not a new panel run or a demand to rewrite an accepted piece.

Send only material outcomes to the field-notes log. Classify the smallest owner, explain the observation and human decision, and promote a rule only with adequate editorial reasoning and the owner's authorization. A simulated-panel movement is evidence to inspect, never a rule by itself.

## Record a note when

- Harley materially corrects or rejects the skill's advice;
- an edit improves one dimension but damages another;
- the method misses an important issue;
- restraint or abstention unexpectedly protects the article;
- a new form exposes a gap;
- two instructions conflict;
- a surprising success may generalise;
- the method overcorrects.

Do not log routine invocations or retain full drafts. Record the article or PR, stage, observation, categorical outcome, human correction, scope, likely owner and promotion evidence.

## Classify ownership

- **Article:** true only of this piece.
- **Reference:** reusable editorial technique.
- **Router:** discovery or sequencing failure.
- **Playbook:** repository workflow or publication mechanics.
- **Doctrine:** durable editorial value requiring Harley's explicit decision.
- **Audit:** deterministic observation or source-custody behaviour.

One occurrence normally remains an observation. Recurrence across materially different work may become a candidate for focused reproduction against current live material. Harley may explicitly promote a single well-reasoned lesson after inspecting the article and its evidence. Update the smallest justified authority and recheck affected work.

Never edit the skill merely because the log contains a candidate. Never promote doctrine autonomously. Ask Harley when the decision turns on taste, voice, policy or competing acceptable outcomes.
