# Final Review

Run this after developmental and line edits are settled.

## Read the article several ways

1. Title and standfirst without the body: do they describe the article now present?
2. Headings only: can a scanning reader recover movement?
3. First sentence of every paragraph: do openings orient without becoming schematic?
4. Continuous read aloud: listen for breath, ambiguity, accidental drumbeat and over-joined thought.
5. Final paragraph covered: has the article already ended?

## Inspect the web object

- semantic headings, quotations, code and technical terms;
- accessible and meaningful link text;
- dates, metadata, reading time and related routes;
- figures, pull quotes and asides that earn their interruption;
- responsive rendering and reading measure;
- claims supported by nearby evidence;
- the whole public site's 12A character, including relevant non-language categories.

## Recovery check

Name what improved, what meaning or cadence was damaged, whether supplied language was stronger, whether the result became more generic, and whether a local improvement harmed the whole. Revert changes without defensible reader benefit.

Follow the article-writing playbook for generated surfaces, the tracked commit gate and publication evidence. An editorial pass does not grant authority to publish.
