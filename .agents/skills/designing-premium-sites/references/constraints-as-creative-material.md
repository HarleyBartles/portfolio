# Constraints as creative material

Limitations are not only obstacles; they also clarify what matters. A tight scope, a limited palette, a single typeface, or a strict performance budget can produce a more refined result than unlimited freedom.

Use constraints to force decisions and to prevent the portfolio from becoming a catalogue of disconnected tricks. Constraints make the remaining choices more meaningful.

## $10k vs $500

| $500 | $10k |
|---|---|
| No constraints; everything is possible | Uses constraints to force decisions |
| Grows by adding more | Refines by removing the unnecessary |
| Scope drifts | Scope is explicit and defended |
