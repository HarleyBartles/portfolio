# Clarity of purpose

The portfolio exists to present the engineer as a capable professional. Every section should answer "what do I want the reader to think or do next?"

A premium portfolio is not a showcase of every technology the author has ever touched; it is a curated argument for the author's judgment. Each project, each paragraph, and each call to action should move the reader toward that goal.

## $10k vs $500

| $500 | $10k |
|---|---|
| Shows every project and technology | Curates a clear argument for the author's judgment |
| Sections with no clear job | Every section has a single intended outcome |
| "About me" instead of "why hire me" | Each paragraph moves the reader toward a goal |
