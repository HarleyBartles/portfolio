# Foundation principles for premium portfolios

These principles come from two shared threads in *Resilient Web Design* and *The Shape of Design*: the web is a resilient medium, and design is the intentional shaping of relationships between people and objects.

## Resilience

A premium portfolio is built in layers. The base experience works without JavaScript, fancy fonts, or perfect networks. Enhancements are progressive, not assumptions.

## Hierarchy

The eye should be told where to go. Contrast in size, weight, colour, and space creates a clear order. Every element competes for attention; only a few should win.

## Restraint

More options, more animation, more colour, more fonts are not better. Restraint is the discipline to remove what does not serve the core message. A premium feel often comes from fewer, better choices.

## Clarity of purpose

The portfolio exists to present the engineer as a capable professional. Every section should answer "what do I want the reader to think or do next?" If it does not, it is clutter.

## Craft and finish

Details accumulate. Spacing, alignment, type rendering, hover states, and loading behaviour all signal whether the site was assembled or crafted. Treat finish as a feature.
