# Craft and finish

Details accumulate. Spacing, alignment, type rendering, hover states, and loading behaviour all signal whether the site was assembled or crafted.

Treat finish as a feature. The space between elements, the crispness of a focus ring, the way a button responds to a hover, and the absence of layout shift as a font loads are the signals that the work was made with care.

## $10k vs $500

| $500 | $10k |
|---|---|
| Misaligned, inconsistent spacing | Spacing and alignment snap to a scale and a grid |
| Hover and focus states are default or missing | Hover, focus, and loading states are considered and consistent |
| Layout shifts as assets load | No cumulative layout shift; the page feels stable |
