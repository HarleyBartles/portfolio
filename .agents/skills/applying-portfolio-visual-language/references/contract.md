# Portfolio visual-language contract

This is the durable non-home presentation authority for the portfolio. Apply it
with `.agents/doctrine/portfolio-design-policy.md`; do not reconstruct Phase 8P
history or treat old proof compositions as templates.

## Governing direction

The visual territory is **Working Engineering Record**: serious engineering,
visibly handled by a human.

Authorship comes from judgement, revision, evidence, provenance and custody. It
does not come from decorative signs of hand-making. Avoid both recognisable
generative-editorial defaults and performative anti-AI craft theatre. Every
conspicuous house choice should be explainable from the actual material, project
structure, evidence or editorial job.

## Shared substrate and project colour

- The default non-home substrate is cool mineral `#E6EAEB`. It is neutral ground,
  not brand colour, and should remain visibly dominant.
- Project identity comes from project-native colour, not a universal house accent.
- The default project-colour authority is **bounded bleed**: project-owned colour
  may influence a finite meaningful area, but the field visibly terminates and
  returns to the mineral substrate.
- `Bounded` describes authority, not shape. Do not turn it into a repeated tinted
  rectangle or route-wide project theme.
- Patch may contribute controlled teal, calm competence, bounded workflow,
  readable hierarchy and deliberate consistency. It does not contribute mascot
  geometry, cartoon shading, rounded-card language, teal title bars or invented
  Patch-like imagery.
- Wild Bunch may contribute earth colour, spatial modularity and constructed-world
  structure. Temporary development colours are not palette authority.
- The shared quiet classification token is evidence-group secondary `#B8C2AD`.
  It may distinguish one subordinate logical group inside bounded evidence when
  project-native colour would be misleading. It is not a site accent, status
  colour, project identity colour or recurring component treatment.

## Spatial grammar

The rectilinear grid is the grammar. Alignment is the default.

Evidence or project-owned material may break one local grid relationship when
its content genuinely earns the interruption. Breaks must be bounded, legible
and rare. There is no quota for asymmetry and no decorative "interesting
layout" slot.

### Rail and field

Rail + field is a sanctioned special case, not a route skeleton. Use a rail only
when a narrow, semantically distinct information stream genuinely benefits from
staying beside broader material, such as chronology, state, classification,
constraints or custody.

A rail must carry information, belong only to the span it serves, avoid stealing
width from primary material, avoid becoming a branded stripe, and collapse into
ordinary semantic source order at narrow widths. If removing it loses only
composition and no information architecture, do not use it.

## Evidence and imagery custody

Artifact-first is the default. Evidence keeps its source-native visual boundary.
The portfolio establishes custody through adjacency, ordering, captions,
provenance, status and qualification rather than laundering unlike receipts into
one universal component.

Do not create a shared evidence header/body/footer module merely for consistency.
Code, screenshots, diagrams and documents may remain visibly different kinds of
evidence. A local custody seam is allowed when provenance or qualification is
independently important enough to deserve parallel reading and doing so does not
damage the evidence.

Purposeful cropping is allowed when surrounding chrome, empty field or unrelated
UI is not part of the evidence. Preserve source custody and make the crop's
editorial purpose clear.

The approved Wild Bunch image-edge primitive is a sparse, irregular
tintype/old-photographic-plate density-loss edge. It is project-specific and must
not become a clean CSS feather, torn paper, burnt map, fake sepia or scrapbook
effect.

## Rhythm and density

Space expresses relationship. Material belonging to one claim may form a tighter
reading cluster; a real change of argument, subject or story movement earns more
separation.

Project/evidence-heavy routes may tighten proof around the claim it qualifies.
Authored longform retains a stable reading pulse rather than inheriting project
compression. Professional and heterogeneous routes vary cadence according to
semantic grouping rather than uniform module spacing.

Rules are subordinate to negative space. Use a rule to reinforce a real boundary,
not to manufacture hierarchy. Exact spacing values are implementation decisions
unless they change perceived character or semantic grouping.

## Shared shell and navigation

The masthead owns site identity and primary navigation only. Current-route
context belongs to the page.

The shared shell uses one identity/navigation flow. Do not split it into folio,
breadcrumb, route-context or split-index rows merely to look publication-like.
Primary routes remain Projects, Writing, Patch, About and CV, with the HB mark
linking Home. Visible `Harley Bartles` beside the HB mark is part of the accepted
quiet shell.

At narrow widths the same navigation flow may wrap naturally. Do not invent a
second responsive hierarchy without a real comprehension or accessibility need.
Preserve authoritative deep links/history, correct top or fragment arrival,
Back/Forward scroll restoration, and correct focus/accessibility state. Direct
deep links must not manufacture a homepage transition the visitor never
experienced.

## Human intervention boundary

Interior routes do not perform humanity through decorative disorder. Do not add
faux handwriting, scribbled margins, tape, scrapbook collage, torn-paper tricks,
fake corrections or generic handmade texture.

Human interventions should be extremely sparse and evidence-led. A real
correction, diff or plain-language technical annotation may work because the
content earns it. If an intervention is predictable as a component slot, it has
become a gimmick.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

The visual language must survive real content at desktop, narrow/mobile and 200%
browser zoom. Preserve:

- readable hierarchy without clipped or colliding display text;
- ordinary semantic source order when desktop rails/asymmetries collapse;
- no horizontal page-level overflow caused by the visual system;
- evidence that remains readable and appropriately scrollable when native
  content genuinely requires it;
- visible keyboard focus and ordinary link semantics;
- the typography skill's metadata floors and type roles;
- sufficient contrast for text, rules, captions, active navigation and
  project-colour bleed; and
- sensible missing-media behaviour rather than decorative placeholders.

## AI-convergence valuation gate

If a credible cultural reference can identify the delivered visual language
primarily as a common AI-design aesthetic, the result fails the portfolio's
valuation gate. The remedy is not mechanical inversion. Identity should come
from material custody, semantic typography, project-native colour, disciplined
geometry and selective evidence-led interruption, not from a generic
cream/rust/huge-serif/tracked-label/rounded-component cluster or an anti-AI
collage cliché.

A technically compliant result may still fail if local composition turns this
contract into a recognisable generic template.
