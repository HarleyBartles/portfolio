# Article-Writing Field Notes

Status: non-binding evidence log
Owner: Portfolio repository
Scope: material lessons from using the Portfolio article-writing method
Authority: this document records evidence and candidates; it does not create instructions, policy or editorial authority
Nearest router: `/AGENTS.md`

## Purpose

Record the outcomes that may help the article-writing skill improve over time. Consult these notes when a current article presents a relevant problem, but verify every candidate against the article in front of you and the current writing doctrine.

Do not add an entry after every invocation. Record a note only for a meaningful correction, rejection, mixed outcome, missed issue, surprising success, useful abstention, new form, conflict or overcorrection.

## Promotion

```text
field use
→ human correction or observed success
→ candidate lesson
→ classify the likely owner
→ reproduce against current live work when useful
→ update the smallest authority
→ recheck affected work
```

One occurrence is recorded, not promoted. Recurrence across materially different work may justify a focused review. A clear conflict with existing authority requires repair to the smallest owning authority and a recheck of affected work. Doctrine changes only through Harley's explicit editorial decision. The skill does not rewrite itself.

Consolidate this log when it gets in the way of use. Do not invent a time, entry-count or file-size trigger.

## Entry template

### Candidate: concise name

- **Article or PR:** path, URL or identifier
- **Editorial stage:** commission, design, draft, macro edit, line edit, final review or publication
- **Observation:** what happened
- **Outcome:** improved, improved with damage, changed but not improved, worse or appropriately abstained
- **Human correction:** what Harley accepted, rejected or reframed
- **Scope:** article-specific, form-specific, recurring or system-wide
- **Candidate owner:** article, skill reference, skill router, playbook, doctrine or audit tooling
- **Promotion evidence:** none yet, or links to materially different examples and the promoting commit or PR

Explain enough to recover the editorial lesson without retaining a phrase bank, imitation corpus, full before-and-after draft or synthetic model of Harley's voice.
