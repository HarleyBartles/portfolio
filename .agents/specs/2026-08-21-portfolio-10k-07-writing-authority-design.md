## Goal

Turn the portfolio's current five-article writing floor into a finished,
deliberately navigable body of technical writing that demonstrates Harley's
authority as a senior software engineer with a distinctive agentic-engineering
edge.

The phase must prove breadth without pretending that volume is authority. Two
articles must make core engineering judgment inspectable. Three may lead with
agentic engineering only because they expose different layers of that field:
professional position, operating environment, and governed process.

The governing thesis is:

> A passing test proves only the kind of claim it was designed to examine.
> Authority comes from matching evidence to risk, explaining the boundary of
> the result, and making the reasoning useful to another engineer.

The writing-system thesis is:

> Five essays should feel like one authored publication with several ways in,
> not five adjacent blog posts or a thin chronological feed.

## Outcome

Phase 7 will deliver:

- five fully groomed and author-approved public essays;
- two core-engineering arguments and three distinct agentic-engineering
  arguments;
- a shared premium editorial essay system with article-specific proof figures;
- a writing-specific manifest contract for editorial datelines, reading time,
  index leadership, homepage-feature readiness, visual identity, and curated
  continuations;
- one deliberate lead essay on the writing index;
- homepage-feature inputs for every published essay without implementing the
  Phase 8 homepage selection mechanic;
- exactly two authored continuation choices on every essay;
- no automatic chronological, random, or first-available article fallback;
- a real Graph-versus-Context evidence gate with an approved Plan B;
- explicit lineage for skill TDD, the Marketplace extension, and later-found
  frontier evaluation guidance;
- a source-custody record covering conceptual lineage and supporting evidence;
- continued asset-custody records for every copied or transformed visual;
- responsive, accessible article figures without a runtime chart system or
  MDX dependency;
- clear failure and fallback behaviour; and
- automated and manual validation suitable for JIT implementation planning.

Phase 7 will not:

- publish six essays merely to make the catalogue look larger;
- preserve rough placeholder prose because it already exists;
- turn the writing index into a search, topic taxonomy, filter system, or
  publication-scale archive before the content volume earns one;
- build a homepage article feed or select the homepage feature;
- render whole essays inline on the homepage;
- implement RSS, `datePublished`, social-sharing metadata, or publication
  chronology owned by Phase 9;
- describe an editorial dateline as `published`, `posted`, or `written on`;
- claim that a public article's editorial dateline matches its Git commit or
  deployment date;
- create five bespoke microsites or introduce MDX for article-specific layout;
- add a runtime graph, chart, or diagram dependency;
- use generic AI imagery, stock imagery, or decorative diagrams to simulate
  authority;
- present a planned review graph as implemented or trustworthy;
- expose private Marketplace branches, dirty checkout state, proprietary
  Access details, customer identities, candidate data, or internal metrics;
- claim Harley holds a senior title he does not hold;
- discuss job-search motives, dissatisfaction, recognition, or employer
  evaluation in the Access-grounded article;
- claim Harley invented skill TDD or any other method derived from
  `obra/superpowers`;
- rewrite later-discovered sources as influences that preceded the work;
- treat common ownership of two repositories as sufficient attribution;
- add an interactive proof surface owned by Phase 11; or
- write a JIT implementation plan before this specification is approved.

## Skill-TDD lineage and Marketplace extension

The article must distinguish three facts without false modesty or false
novelty.

### Inherited method

RED/GREEN pressure testing for agent skills is deliberately derived from
`obra/superpowers`. The article must name and link that lineage.

The stable upstream evidence inspected for this design is
`obra/superpowers` revision
`b36e0829c6d0140e93cfef2ca599b1b07d4a7797`, particularly
`skills/writing-skills/testing-skills-with-subagents.md`.

Implementation must refresh and pin the source used in the final article.

### Traditional engineering composition

Harley's custody rule composes familiar engineering principles:

- version the test definition with the claim it examines;
- execute the definition against a particular checkout, runner, configuration,
  and environment;
- treat test reports as observations produced by that run; and
- let another verifier execute the portable specification rather than inherit
  somebody else's result.

The short formulation is:

> Version the test with the claim. Bind the result to the run.

The public-facing formulation is:

> The scenario travels with the skill. The result stays with the run.

### Harley's extension

Agent Asset Marketplace packages reusable pressure scenarios with the skills
that make behavioural claims. The Marketplace retains the observations from
its own RED/GREEN campaigns. A consumer receives the same question and may run
it against its own model, harness, tools, instructions, and repository state.

The article may say plainly that this exact evidence-custody composition is
Harley's novel extension. It must not claim global priority or invention of
the established ingredients.

### Later independent convergence

Harley did not read the later-cited OpenAI or Anthropic guidance before
implementing this custody rule. Those sources are evidence of independent
convergence with emerging evaluation standards, not original inspiration.

Controlled further reading may include:

- Anthropic, `Demystifying evals for AI agents`;
- OpenAI, `A shared playbook for trustworthy third party evaluations`;
- GitHub Actions documentation for workflow test artifacts;
- Azure Pipelines documentation for published test runs; and
- Pact documentation for version-bound provider verification.

The final article should use the smallest set that advances the argument. A
source-custody record must label each relationship accurately as derivation,
traditional composition, later convergence, supporting evidence, or further
reading.

## Editorial completion standard

Except for the closed vibe-coding argument, existing prose is source material,
not protected text.

Every public essay requires:

- one governing argument expressible in one sentence;
- a concrete opening rather than abstract throat-clearing;
- sections that advance rather than restate the argument;
- real decisions, failure modes, trade-offs, or implementation evidence;
- an honest boundary around what the evidence does not prove;
- a useful takeaway another engineer can apply;
- controlled evidence and lineage links;
- a quiet conclusion rather than a hiring pitch; and
- Harley's final author review.

The senior-candidate proposition remains subtext. Do not end essays with
`therefore I am a senior engineer` or `therefore hire me`.

### Editorial dateline

Replace the ambiguous generic writing `date` with an editorial dateline or an
equivalently explicit internal name.

Public presentation shows the human-formatted date without `Published`,
`Posted`, or `Written on`. It is a soft editorial coordinate. It is not a
promise about the first commit, public deployment, or repository chronology.

Editing or grooming an essay does not automatically update the dateline.

Phase 9 must not silently reuse the dateline as RSS `pubDate`, schema.org
`datePublished`, or another hard publication claim. A true publication field
would be a separate fact with separate evidence.

### Writing-index leadership

`indexLead` replaces the writing meaning currently overloaded into `featured`.
Exactly one published essay leads the writing index. The initial lead remains
the vibe-coding essay.

Writing-index prominence and homepage eligibility are independent decisions.
The remaining index may use the editorial dateline as a stable ordering input,
but the interface must not call that order `latest`, `recent`, or publication
chronology.

### Homepage-feature readiness

Every published essay supplies:

- explicit eligibility;
- a concise homepage proposition written for the feature context;
- a stable visual identity and accessible description; and
- complete canonical-route and reading metadata.

The proposition is not an automatic truncation of the article summary.

Phase 7 makes every launch essay eligible. Phase 8 chooses and composes a
homepage feature from that pool, removes the interim feed-like presentation,
and decides how the feature participates in the wider homepage journey.

Phase 7 must not implement rotation, randomness, chronology, scoring, or any
other homepage-selection mechanic.

### Visual identity

Each essay receives a stable typed visual identifier and a human-readable
description of what its principal figure communicates.

An unknown identifier is a validation failure. Do not render a generic
illustration as fallback.

### Curated continuations

Every published essay declares exactly two continuation records. Each record
contains:

- the target article slug; and
- one public sentence explaining why that article follows.

The rendered choice includes the target title, rationale, reading time, and a
descriptive linked heading.

Do not use a topic label such as `towards core engineering` or `towards
agentic engineering` in the public interface. The semantic branching remains
editorial structure that the reader experiences through interest.

## Source and asset custody

The portfolio is the golden consumer of Harley's wider work. Common ownership
changes permission; it does not remove source identity, licence, transformation,
or attribution obligations.

The rule is:

> Ownership is not provenance, and a permissive licence is not an authorship
> claim.

### Visual asset custody

Continue `docs/asset-custody.md` for every copied or transformed visual. Each
record includes:

- consuming article and visual identifier;
- origin repository and exact source path;
- exact source revision;
- author or owner;
- licence and attribution basis;
- public derivative path and format;
- transformations and optimisation;
- alt text, visible caption, and failure fallback; and
- date added.

Cross-repository Harley-owned media receives the same record as third-party
media. Do not hotlink a moving sibling-repository asset when the portfolio can
hold an optimised, attributable derivative.

### Editorial source custody

Add `docs/editorial-source-custody.md` or an equivalently explicit committed
record for conceptual lineage, quotations, implementation proof, and further
reading.

Each record includes:

- consuming article or figure;
- source title, author or owner, and canonical URL;
- exact repository revision when applicable;
- licence and attribution requirement when text, code, or media is copied or
  adapted;
- usage mode: copied, adapted, quoted, referenced, or linked only;
- relationship: derived from, traditional composition, independent
  convergence, supporting evidence, or further reading; and
- the location of public attribution.

This relationship field prevents later-discovered reading from being rewritten
as original influence.

### Public attribution

- Name conceptual derivation in the prose where the claim matters.
- Give cross-repository media a quiet source credit in the figure caption or
  adjacent provenance note.
- Meet every third-party licence's visible attribution requirements.
- Distinguish `derived from` from `aligned with` or `further reading`.
- Use an exact revision link for implementation proof and an optional moving
  default-branch link for general exploration.
- Do not hide meaningful lineage in a site-wide legal dump.

The skill-TDD article must name Superpowers before describing Harley's
extension. The shared MIT licence makes this distinction more important, not
less important.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

### Semantic structure

- One page-level `h1` identifies the essay.
- Body headings descend without skipped levels.
- The route uses one article landmark.
- Every principal visual uses `figure` and a useful visible `figcaption`.
- Continuations use a labelled navigation landmark.
- The writing index has one clear lead item without making all items appear
  subordinate or inaccessible.

### Equivalent meaning

Diagrams must not depend on colour, geometry, hover, or SVG alone.

Use semantic HTML where possible. Where SVG is required, pair it with an
equivalent visible or screen-reader-accessible ordered description. A caption
must explain the argumentative point, not merely say `diagram`.

### Narrow layouts

- Figures reflow into a meaningful single-column sequence.
- Text and diagrams do not require horizontal scrolling.
- Code blocks may scroll horizontally inside their own boundary.
- Continuation choices stack in authored order.
- No label or annotation is reduced to illegible display text.

### Keyboard and focus

- Reading order and focus order match.
- Every continuation and source link has a descriptive accessible name.
- Focus indication remains visible against the editorial surface.
- No hover-only source, tooltip, or diagram explanation is allowed.

### Motion

No essential motion is required. Any incidental transition must respect
`prefers-reduced-motion` and leave the full static meaning present.

## Performance contract

- Add no runtime chart, graph, or MDX dependency.
- Prefer semantic HTML, CSS, and small owned SVGs.
- Raster assets require an evidential purpose, responsive dimensions, and
  optimisation recorded in custody.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold raster evidence.
- Preserve explicit dimensions to prevent layout shift.
- Keep fonts within the existing self-hosted families.
- Preserve the repository's bundle, asset, and route budgets.
- Essential article content renders from the committed site without fetching
  GitHub or another repository at runtime.

## Failure and fallback behaviour

### Graph gate failure

If public default-branch evidence cannot prove the review graph at JIT time,
select Context and record the decision in the JIT plan. Do not wait indefinitely
or lower the evidence threshold to preserve the preferred title.

Once selected, Graph and Context cannot be silently exchanged during
implementation.

### Continuation failure

A missing, unpublished, duplicated, or self-referential continuation is a
validation failure.

If supporting navigation data fails at runtime despite successful build-time
validation, render a quiet unavailable status. Do not substitute chronological,
random, or first-available links.

### Figure failure

An unregistered visual identifier fails validation. A missing raster asset
retains meaningful caption and text; do not replace it with generic art.

### External-source failure

External links may become unavailable without preventing the core essay from
rendering. The article must remain understandable without following them.

Verify source URLs and exact revisions during grooming and before publication.

### Interim homepage

The existing homepage may retain its interim article presentation until Phase
8. Phase 7 must avoid breaking it, but must not expand it into the final
selection mechanic.

### Obsolete drafts

Unselected or superseded placeholders are not public manifest entries. Apply
cleanup custody during JIT planning: absorb useful prose, retain only a bounded
backlog note when it has future value, and remove disposable duplicate drafts.
Git history remains available.
