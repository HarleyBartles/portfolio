# The Usual Specialists V2: Writ Chapter Design

**Status:** Approved chapter direction; local composition requires further Harley design dialogue before JIT planning

**Parent page spec:** `2026-09-17-usual-specialists-visual-story-design.md`

**Chapter job:** Establish the authority and scope under which the crew may act.

## Settled story contract

Silk has resolved a physically reliable ingress/egress route. That still does not
mean the crew is entitled to use it.

Writ establishes the authority, scope, limits, ownership and mandate envelope
under which action can lawfully happen. He does not choose the operational plan;
Klause does that next.

The chapter must preserve the distinction:

- Silk owns route reliability.
- Writ owns authority.
- Klause owns decision.

## Audience change

The reader should move from "we know how to get in and out" to "we now know the
authority under which action can happen, and where that authority stops."

The chapter should make boundaries consequential rather than decorate the page
with generic legal paperwork.

## Primary visual family and current direction

**Primary family:** frame/surface and visual rhetoric.

**Supporting family:** controlled staging.

After Silk's black comic rupture, Writ should feel like geometry regaining
authority. Frames stabilise. Camera and eyelines become deliberate. Thresholds,
scope, permitted/prohibited relationships and formal closure become the story
material.

This direction is approved. The exact page composition is not yet approved.

## Safehouse territory

Writ has converted two adjoining apartment rooms into genuine law chambers because
of course he has. He has added a frosted door and receptionist space because the
office needs the cachet of the authority it represents.

This is not faux-institutional parody. Writ is literally the arbiter of law around
these parts. The chambers should feel adapted from an ordinary apartment, but the
adaptation is serious, practiced and fully inhabited.

## Settled beats to preserve through design

The detailed composition may change, but these story beats remain useful and
approved:

1. Patch approaches/enters over the shoulder toward the frosted threshold,
   carrying the accumulated Index + Silk folder state.
2. Writ is already doing rounds/work while Patch pitches.
3. Writ reaches the precise stop: `Absolutely not. On whose authority?`
4. The chapter resolves authority/scope rather than retesting the route.
5. Writ's pink carbon-copy assent instrument becomes the closing hero and joins the
   folder state.
6. Writ returns to his rounds with procedural finality.

A peripheral Receipt filing-copy action may survive as foreshadowing only if it is
almost incidental and does not become Writ's closing event.

## Copy and medium

Writ earns language through frosted-door identity, forms, signatures, procedural
notation, scope language and one editorial story card.

Do not use `make the route lawful` as the core explanation. Silk already resolved
the route. Writ establishes the authority under which a later chosen plan may use
what Silk proved.

Do not make Writ's environment another dense archive. Paperwork density is Index's
territory; Writ's language is boundary, validity, scope and formal relationship.

## Assent and exit contract

The pink carbon-copy instrument is the narrative answer. Writ's immediate return to
rounds/review is the character answer.

No celebration, recruitment pose or generic `I'M IN` confirmation is needed.

## Responsive survival requirement

Whatever composition is approved later must preserve:

- arrival through a real chamber threshold;
- clear separation of route reliability from authority;
- a decisive authority/scope stop and resolution;
- the outgoing Writ folder state;
- formal/stable visual character after Silk; and
- comprehension at 320 CSS px, actual 200% zoom and reduced motion.

Responsive translation need not preserve desktop frame count or camera placement.

## Design questions that remain open

These are human-owned composition decisions and must be resolved before JIT
implementation planning:

1. What is the dominant Writ field: one major chambers tableau with subordinate
   legal events, or a short directed sequence of distinct frames?
2. How does authority/scope become visually legible without turning into generic
   forms, stamps or diagrammatic bureaucracy?
3. Which exact chamber beat deserves the largest visual weight: arrival,
   `On whose authority?`, or the authority resolution?
4. What is the exact staging of the carbon-copy assent and accumulated folder at
   the close?
5. Does Receipt's filing-copy cameo materially improve the later Receipt reveal,
   and if so how peripheral should it be?
6. How exactly does Silk's black field terminate into Writ's frosted/formal
   threshold without making the transition itself the chapter?

## Non-goals

- Do not make Writ choose the plan.
- Do not make Writ re-prove Silk's route.
- Do not make the chambers a joke bureaucracy or generic corporate office.
- Do not copy Index's paperwork density.
- Do not make Receipt a co-star.
- Do not commission final Writ art before the composition questions above are
  settled and the live wireframe proves the chosen structure.
