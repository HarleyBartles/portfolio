# The Usual Specialists V2: Silk Chapter Design

**Status:** Settled design carried forward from the Harley-approved 17 September 2026 visual-story specification; ready for chapter-level planning after refactor review

**Parent page spec:** `2026-09-17-usual-specialists-visual-story-design.md`

**Chapter question:** What survives contact with reality?

## Story job

Silk inherits several plausible routes and constraints from Index and pressure-tests
them against the actual building. She may discard whole candidates and combine
surviving fragments from more than one. Her output is one reliable ingress/egress
route: this is how the crew gets in and out without getting burned.

She does **not** resolve the operational plan. Klause does that later.

## Audience change

The reader should move from "there are several plausible ways through" to "this
route has survived hostile reality." The chapter must visibly contain failure and
adaptation; a sequence of scenic corridor views is not enough.

## Primary visual family and viewing contract

**Primary family:** encounter/sequence.

**Supporting families:** frame/surface and internal-frame/occlusion.

**Viewing contract:** directed. The causal beats should be difficult to read out of
order without losing meaning.

Index lets the reader browse. Silk takes control of the reader for one chapter.

## Safehouse territory and substrate

The physical location remains the apartment corridor and its service
infrastructure: cheap doors, floorboards, conduit, voids, anchors and hidden routes.

The chapter representation is explicitly black/dark comic-book territory. This
supersedes the 12 September mineral-page-as-plaster direction.

This is not arbitrary chapter theming. The homepage Specialists movement already
uses torn page edges to reveal a dark/black comic substrate beneath the polite web
surface. Silk is the chapter that cashes that cheque in the full story.

The surface contract is nested:

1. the portfolio normally presents a polite shared surface;
2. the Index-to-Silk transition tears into the dark comic substrate;
3. comic panels establish a second local rule; and
4. Silk breaks that local rule once.

The chapter violates the site surface. Silk violates the chapter surface.

Transparent Silk is therefore scarce. Use one canonical breakout only. If she is
continually floating above frames, the transgression has no value.

## Beat map

1. **Inheritance.** Patch arrives in Silk's test corridor carrying Index's folded
   blueprint/map material and yellow note. Silk receives candidate routes, not a
   blank mission.
2. **Test.** Candidate assumptions meet the actual corridor, doors, floorboards,
   anchors, service voids and other constraints.
3. **Concrete failure.** At least one proposed route or assumption visibly fails.
4. **Refusal / breach.** The prescribed boundary no longer works. Silk creates the
   chapter's canonical rupture and stops obeying the panel/page rule.
5. **Hidden traversal.** The new path moves through the building's service guts.
   Rope/red cord behaves as functional route and force vector, not decoration.
6. **Resolution / realisation.** Surviving route fragments are recombined into one
   dependable ingress/egress route. Silk's surprised-eyes beat is the preferred
   reaction because it carries the exact character recognition: she has thrown
   everything at this route and, damn, it held. That is her internal `you son of a
   gun` moment; the marker carries the recurring wording materially.
7. **Assent aftermath.** The Silk marker is already on the folder and Silk has
   already moved on.

## Closing frame contract

The camera is with Patch, slightly behind and to one side, looking down the
corridor.

Patch is partial foreground rather than a portrait. The folder is readable enough
to show:

- Index's folded blue material;
- Index's yellow note; and
- Silk's newly attached circular route marker with its red-cord relationship.

The hierarchy is:

1. fresh Silk marker;
2. its physical relationship to Index's prior material; and
3. Silk already far down the corridor on a zipwire toward another off-frame job.

The handoff happened a beat ago. Patch may still be settling the marker. Silk does
not wait for thanks.

A comic caption such as `Apparently that means she said yes.` is earned if the
image already makes the assent clear and the caption adds character rather than
explaining mechanics.

## Existing Silk material: reuse is conditional

The current live Silk composition is not design authority. Its strongest images
may survive only when they serve this causal beat map.

Likely reuse/reference candidates:

- corridor world;
- service-corridor imagery;
- transparent hands-free abseil/traversal Silk as the one breakout; and
- surprised-eyes insert as the approved realisation beat if the existing asset
  still carries the required restrained surprise.

Expected superseded composition authority:

- repeated plaster aperture rims;
- Receipt peek-through subplot;
- equal-weight scenic apertures;
- decorative re-occlusion justified only by alpha layers;
- Commission 09 knockthrough/stand-in ending; and
- old geometry helpers whose purpose exists only for that discarded arrangement.

Accepted old masters and provenance remain in custody even when no longer rendered.

## Composition and hierarchy

Silk should probably use fewer major elements than Index. Index's density expresses
competence; Silk's competence is force and decisiveness.

Teach containment first. Then break the rule once.

The black field is bounded to Silk and must visibly terminate at the fold into
Writ. It is not permission to create a dark route shell or themed navigation.

The rope/red cord must do physical work: anchoring, tension, route, movement or
marker continuity. It must not become a page-wide decorative line.

## Copy

Silk earns comic captions and sound/annotation language when they live naturally
inside the comic medium. One editorial story card remains allowed.

Do not use prose to narrate which candidate failed, where she moved or that she
accepted the job when the sequence can show it.

## Responsive survival contract

Preserve:

**inheritance → test → failure → breach → hidden traversal → reliable route →
assent aftermath**.

Do not preserve desktop panel count merely because it existed at 1440px. Narrow
layouts may merge or remove subordinate inserts, but they must keep:

- a clear inherited Index state;
- one real failed assumption;
- one canonical breakout;
- the sense of traversal through hidden infrastructure;
- a reliable-route resolution; and
- the final corridor/folder aftermath.

At actual 200% zoom and reduced motion the before/after states must remain legible
without parallax or animation.

## Non-goals

- Do not use mineral/plaster as Silk's dominant substrate.
- Do not make every frame an aperture.
- Do not make Receipt a Silk subplot.
- Do not use transparent Silk as default styling.
- Do not make Silk prove "Index's route" right or wrong.
- Do not let Silk resolve the overall caper plan.
- Do not preserve old composition because the assets were expensive.
- Do not let final generated art dictate route-level geometry.

## Chapter acceptance criteria

- [ ] The chapter reads causally before final imagery is installed.
- [ ] At least one candidate assumption visibly fails.
- [ ] Exactly one meaningful frame/page breakout occurs.
- [ ] The black comic field is finite and clearly ends before Writ.
- [ ] Silk resolves a reliable ingress/egress route, not the operational plan.
- [ ] The closing corridor frame shows Index continuity plus the new Silk marker.
- [ ] Silk's realisation reads as impressed surprise that the route survived her
      pressure, not as generic recruitment enthusiasm.
- [ ] Silk is already leaving for other work in the closing read.
- [ ] Old accepted Silk assets that leave the render retain honest custody.
