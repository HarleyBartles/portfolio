# The Sorcerer's Apprentice — Delegation Without Boundaries

Delegation needs a boundary, not just permission.

This Patch Fairytale follows the Sorcerer's Apprentice pattern: a sensible five-worker handoff, a magnified look at the missing instruction that caused the trouble, the runaway consequence, and the Delegation Policy prop that fixes it. The failure is not delegation itself; it is delegation without a boundary.

![A bounded five-worker delegation expands into an uncontrolled crowd before a delegation policy restores limits.](/fairytales/sorcerers-apprentice/page-1200.webp)

## Visual transcript

1. Patch gives a task to five workers beside a desk of source material. The intended delegation is small and understandable.
2. A magnified instruction sheet grants broad authority, including permission to hire anyone else needed. It does not define depth or worker limits.
3. Coloured delegation paths multiply into a crowd of workers carrying tools, documents, and supplies. The work has escaped its original boundary.
4. A Delegation Policy limits the task, direct delegates, delegation depth, tools and data, and escalation behaviour.

**The lesson:** Delegation needs a boundary, not just permission. Tell agents both what authority they have and how far they may delegate it.
