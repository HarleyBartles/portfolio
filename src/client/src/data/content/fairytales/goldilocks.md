# Goldilocks — The Right Amount of Guidance

Good agent guidance is not maximum context, but enough relevant context for a confident next decision.

This Patch Fairytale shows three versions of the same route choice: too much guidance, not enough, and just right. Too much becomes a burden. Too little leaves too many options. The right amount is curated, relevant, and lets Patch take the next step with confidence.

![Three scenes compare too much, too little, and just enough guidance for Patch.](/fairytales/goldilocks/page-1200.webp)

## Visual transcript

1. **Too much:** Patch stands at a woodland junction buried in maps, tools, notes, rules, and competing signposts. The excess guidance has become another problem to solve.
2. **Not enough:** Patch faces several equally plausible paths with only a vague question mark. Nothing identifies which next move matters.
3. **Just right:** Patch works at an organised desk with a map, plan, checklists, references, and clearly labelled materials. The relevant structure supports a confident decision.

**The lesson:** Good agent guidance is not maximum context. It is enough relevant context for a confident next decision.
