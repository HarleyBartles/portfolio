## Original commission intent

Create a small context-free transparent material kit that lets Opening, Index and Silk each author local rope geometry while the reader experiences one continuous physical red route rope. The source assets were to encode material identity only: consistent red value, braid/fibre, diameter, restrained wear and believable weight, with no chapter coordinates, anchors, plaster, characters or finished route paths.

## Material changes from original intent

The original brief expected two or three pieces if sufficient. Four looser variants were accepted first, then the user requested more tensioned options and three additional taut pieces were generated. The accepted library therefore contains seven masters rather than the minimum kit initially imagined.

## Accepted execution brief

Evidence status: `recovered-from-linear-record`.

Generate isolated slender red rigging/paracord pieces on genuine alpha with generous extra length and neutral overlap-capable ends. Preserve one material family and physical weight; use modest irregularity rather than a perfect vector stroke. No anchor hardware, plaster, brick, aperture, character, typography, route-map graphics, glow or environment. Include loose working bends plus tensioned alternatives and one terminal/curl family so React can compose chapter-owned paths without non-uniform stretching.

- Material authority: approved Silk rigging/thread identity.
