## Original commission intent

The paper package called for separate physical foreground documents that could occlude the continuous rope and overlap other Index material while remaining independently authored in the web composition. Supporting papers were to stay quieter than the hero carrier and avoid generic paper-confetti clutter.

## Material changes from original intent

The accepted second large spill became warm cream engineering graph paper covered in iterative graphite working rather than another old blueprint/folded document. This gave the paper stack a distinct “working the problem” material role while preserving the blue carrier’s unique colour identity.
