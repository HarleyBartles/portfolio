## Original commission intent

Establish one ordinary, slightly tired apartment temporarily appropriated as the safehouse, with Patch entering at the threshold and the corridor providing spatial continuity into the Specialist chapters. The early brief expected canonical Patch, the recruitment folder, useful edge room for the route, and a wide editorial establishing composition. It explicitly rejected a bunker, sci-fi headquarters, labelled dollhouse, or explanatory typography baked into the art.

## Material changes from original intent

Live art direction moved Patch to an extreme rear foreground framing fragment rather than a readable three-quarter character: no faceplate, no bag, strap only. The room became the sharp subject; Patch and the door jamb sit soft on the near plane. The accepted run also removed the generated rope/anchor requirement entirely. The accepted apartment is deliberately less cosy, curated, or resident-specific than the first-turn brief.

## Accepted execution brief

Evidence status: `recovered-from-linear-record`.

Create the opening image from immediately behind Patch as he crosses into one fungible apartment safehouse. Keep only the rear raised teal hood, antennae, shoulder/upper torso fragment and diagonal strap in the extreme-left near plane; no faceplate, bag, legs, or completed body. Keep Patch and the jamb slightly soft, while the worn corridor and apartment depth are sharp. Use matte unfinished floorboards, a shabby runner, bare practical light, neutral temporary-use furnishing and the Adventures of Patch editorial-comic language. Generate no rope or anchor.

The next run became the accepted master.
