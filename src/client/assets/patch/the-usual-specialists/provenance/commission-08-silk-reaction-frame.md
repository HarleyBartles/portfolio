## Original commission intent

Create a narrow horizontal foreground frame for Silk's reaction/eyes aperture using believable traditional lath-and-plaster construction. Old plaster has failed and crumbled away; thin horizontal timber laths are exposed; top and bottom are principally defined by horizontal lath; left and right are irregular snapped lath ends and crumbled plaster; the centre is a transparent horizontal slit; the exterior is transparent. Timber must read as old structural lath rather than boards, beams, modern battens or decorative carpentry.
