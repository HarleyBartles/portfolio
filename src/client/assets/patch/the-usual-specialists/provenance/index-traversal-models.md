## Original commission intent

The Index document world needed several tiny reusable full-body character silhouettes so the page could show Index physically traversing giant documents without baking her into the base plate. Every source was to remain a complete body on genuine transparency, with no paper, environment, prop terrain, crop, occlusion or scene shadow; the web composition would decide what later hides behind documents.

## Material changes from original intent

The set was intentionally diversified after the pilot rather than repeating five walking poses. The accepted families became ordinary traversal, low inspection, high step over implied terrain, reach/balance, and rear-three-quarter progression. The pilot established scale/rendering control; later poses inherited that standard.

## Accepted execution brief

Evidence status: `normalized-from-approved-conversation`.

Generate one full-body Index per image with genuine alpha and breathing room from bun/antenna through both shoes. Preserve compact autonomous-agent proportions, cream faceplate/circular eye language, indigo pencil-held bun, one mostly concealed antenna, heavy brown cardigan, beige blouse, charcoal trousers and grounded shoes. Keep the mood focused, friendly and absorbed rather than dramatic. Use the accepted Index sheet as identity authority and the accepted Commission 03 treatment as chapter-style control. Do not manufacture paper occlusion into the PNG; every pose must remain complete so downstream layers can conceal it honestly.

## Iteration history

`index-walk` was generated and reviewed first as the style-and-scale pilot. Live mounting established that a tiny full-body character remained identifiable across dark blue and pale paper and could survive meaningful downstream occlusion. The user then approved four distinct follow-up silhouette families rather than four more walks: low inspection, high step, reach/balance, and rear progression. Their exact generation tool payload later became unrecoverable, but the resulting PNGs and acceptance pass survived.

## Acceptance decision

Accepted by Harley Bartles on 2026-09-10. The pilot was explicitly locked after tiny-scale page QA; models 2–5 were then stood up across different paper surfaces/overlap conditions and accepted as reusable assets. Acceptance covered the character files, not their temporary x/y positions, sequence or future choreography.

## Known accepted limitations

- The pilot’s antenna bobble is slightly more explicit than ideal, but acceptable at traversal scale.
- Generation IDs/tool payloads for the five Index assets are unavailable from retained history.
- `index-reach-balance` remains accepted historical material outside Portfolio source custody.

Image pixels own the complete character and intrinsic pose only. React owns scale, crop, paper/world placement, rotation, z-order, overlap, masking-by-other-page-elements and responsive choreography. Do not crop/occlude the source master to simulate document interaction.
