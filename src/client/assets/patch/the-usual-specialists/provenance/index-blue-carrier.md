## Original commission intent

The Index foreground-paper package existed to place physical alpha-isolated evidence in front of the route rope and scene/base layers. Its hero left-edge document was expected to provide real paper overlap, a calm title region and independent responsive positioning without baking page typography into image pixels.

## Material changes from original intent

The generic old evidence-sheet carrier evolved into the deliberately unique strong muted-blue sheet. Immediate companion papers stayed cream/grey so the carrier could identify Index materially without becoming a plaque or UI card.
