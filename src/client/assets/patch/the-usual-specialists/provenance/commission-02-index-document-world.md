## Original commission intent

Introduce Index as a friendly, absorbed provenance specialist in a densely used archive room while Patch follows. The first-turn brief used a high three-quarter archive-table camera and treated room, Index, Patch, paper props and later title treatment as one broader chapter-opening composition.

## Material changes from original intent

The accepted concept deliberately discarded the room-first scene. The chapter begins already inside the document world: a direct-overhead, effectively orthographic giant blueprint/document plane is the terrain. Index and Patch became separate transparent tiny traversal assets and the surrounding apartment was reduced to incidental perimeter evidence.

## Accepted execution brief

Evidence status: `recovered-from-linear-record`.

Create a true top-down working-document terrain dominated by one huge aged technical blueprint that runs beyond the frame. Use restrained secondary papers and only tiny perimeter table/room cues. Keep central geometry rich enough for later traversal, top-right comparatively calm, and bottom-right expendable for responsive loss. Do not include Index, Patch, rope, title carrier, final route answer or readable story labels. Match the matte editorial material language established by Commission 01 rather than cinematic prop photography.
